### Corrections registered 2026-08-12
- **`bay2021trace` and `bay2021desert` are the same paper and both are cited in `main.tex`.** Crossref gives
  the seventh author as **Stefan K. Arndt**; `bay2021trace` says "Sandra K. Arndt" and is wrong. Consolidate to
  one key (requires a manuscript edit — not done).
- **Bay 2021's ">70%" is for *inorganic energy sources*, not trace gases.** The trace-gas-specific figure is
  "bacteria from 19 phyla". Citing ">70% of soil taxa encode trace-gas enzymes" is a mis-citation.
- **Delgado-Baquerizo networks paper is 2020, not 2019** — *Land Degrad Dev* 31(3):346–355, doi:10.1002/ldr.3453
  (Crossref "issued" 2019 is online-first). Key added as `delgadobaquerizo2020networks`, not the previously
  promised `delgadobaquerizo2019networks`.
- **`contamination2025guidelines` is first-authored by Fierer** (30-author consensus statement, Greening last),
  so a narrative citation is "Fierer et al. 2025", not an anonymous consensus statement.
- **`ji2017atmospheric` is cited in §2.2 but has never been read** (paywalled, no PDF). See TO_DOWNLOAD.md.
- **Working route to OA PMC PDFs from this node:** `https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC<id>?pdf=render` via curl
  succeeds where `ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc` returns 403. The Europe PMC REST API
  (`ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/...`) also serves abstracts, full-text XML and verified metadata.

### Corrections registered 2026-08-18 (citation audit, session 8)
- **`richardson2023mgnify` does NOT support "MGnify90 is dominated by aquatic and engineered biomes"**
  (`main.tex` L210). Richardson p.D754 says over half of MGnify's analyses come from nine biomes led by
  human-associated, plus marine, **soil**, mammalian digestive and mixed; "engineered" is not among them,
  and the count is of analyses, not protein-database sequences. The "aquatic" idea descends from June's
  biome-of-*hits* breakdown (83.5%), which the workbench records as not recomputable. Fix is a rewrite.
- **`uritskiy2019atacama` is halite (salt-rock) endoliths, not desert soil**, and is one of five comparator
  legs (6.19M of 147.5M seqs). It cannot alone support §3.1's five-comparator sentence, its "largest desert
  soil collections available" superlative, or the "no Arabian or Saharan site" negative. Byline defect:
  Crossref gives **Getsin, Samantha** and **Glass, Brian**; the bib has Samuel and Benjamin.
- **`matchado2024limits` has no soil data.** Human gut disease cohorts plus simulation; its soil remark is a
  second-hand summary of Djemiel et al. (its ref 45). Co-cite `sun2020inference` wherever the claim concerns
  soil-microbiome inference (L168, L466, L438).
- **`bay2021aridity` is a third distinct Bay 2021 key** — *ISME J* 15:3339--3356, doi:10.1038/s41396-021-01001-0,
  Negev, PRJNA657906 — and must not be merged with `bay2021trace` / `bay2021desert` (*Nat Microbiol* 6:246--256).
- **bioRxiv's DOI prefix changed to `10.64898` for late-2025 preprints.** Aplakidou 2025 is
  `10.64898/2025.12.21.695754`; the `10.1101/...` form in `main.tex`'s `\todo` does not resolve.
- **Owed keys, all Crossref-verified, to be inserted by the main session:** `bay2021aridity`,
  `lennon2018relicdna` (*mBio* 9(3):e00637-18, doi:10.1128/mbio.00637-18 — distinct from `lennon2011seedbanks`),
  `aplakidou2025quadrupling`. `dcosta2011ancient` and `vangoethem2018antarctic` were added by the main session
