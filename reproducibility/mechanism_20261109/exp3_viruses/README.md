## 2. Scientific Interpretation Boundaries & Unsupported Claims

1. **Historical Spacer Exposure vs Active Infection**: CRISPR spacer matches and competitive read recruitment measure sequence-linked historical exposure and sample co-presence conditional on sequence ownership ambiguity. They do **not** establish active infection, ongoing lytic replication, host mortality, ecological causality, or recombination.
2. **Sequence Polymorphism, Depth-Qualified Positions & Variant Count Semantics vs Recombination**:
   - Conditional `inStrain` profiling reports read-covered positions (`round(length * breadth)`) and inferred depth-qualified positions (`round(length * breadth_minCov)` at `min_cov = 5`), which are necessary but not sufficient for full variant callability (which also requires base quality, read-pair ANI, and allele frequency/FDR filters). Variants (`SNV_count`, `SNS_count`) are strictly sequence polymorphism / consensus differences and are **never** interpreted as recombination evidence.
   - In `inStrain 1.9.1`, `scaffold_info.tsv` (`calc_snps` in `inStrain/profile/snv_utilities.py:249-272`) counts the last `cumulative_snv_table.csv.gz` record per position at `mm <= max_mm` **without** filtering `cryptic == True` sites, whereas exported `<prefix>_SNVs.tsv` (`SNVprofile.get_nonredundant_snv_table` in `inStrain/SNVprofile.py:495-510`) filters `cryptic == False` **before** sorting `mm` and deduplicating `(scaffold, position)`. Both counts are verified and reported side-by-side.
3. **Pfam Sequence-Profile HMMs vs Structural Coordinate Search**: `hmmscan` against `Pfam-A.hmm` is sequence-domain annotation, **not** structural coordinate alignment. Structural search is gated on exact amino-acid sequence matches to pre-existing structural models and is explicitly reported as `not_performed_no_exact_sequence_matched_existing_models` (0/262 exact models available).
