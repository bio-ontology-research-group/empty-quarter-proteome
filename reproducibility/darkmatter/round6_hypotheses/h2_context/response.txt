## C1. Primary Locus Eligibility Gate Before KO Aggregation, Unmapped/Unavailable Flank Handling, 3'-Stop Sensitivity Tier, and Complete Flank Closure

### Reviewer & Root Audit Finding
In v2 (`v2_superseded/scripts/03_analyse_conservation.py:103-158`), KOs were aggregated across non-truncated flanks before checking locus window completeness, causing 4 families to have impossible $R_{\text{fam}} > 1.0$ (`1.1`, `1.5`, `1.25`, `1.5`) and 2 `QnotK` families to be false-positive $C_{\text{fam}} = 1$ calls, while `source_file_unavailable` and `coordinate_mismatch` flanks were left as callable (`is_complete = True`) negatives (`noKO`). Moreover, 60.3% of `coordinate_mismatch` flanks on binned contigs shared the exact 3' stop codon and strand with a Bakta CDS (5' start differences between meta-Pyrodigal and bin-trained Bakta).

### Implemented Repair & Code Evidence
1. **7-Category Mutually Exclusive Flank Classification (`scripts/02_extract_neighbourhoods.py:390-519`):**
   - Every flanking slot ($\{-2, -1, +1, +2\}$) is classified into exactly one of 7 categories: `mapped_KO`, `mapped_noKO`, `stop3prime_matched_KO`, `stop3prime_matched_noKO`, `coordinate_mismatch_unmapped`, `source_file_unavailable`, or `contig_edge_truncated`.
   - `source_file_unavailable`, `coordinate_mismatch_unmapped`, and `contig_edge_truncated` are strictly **missing/unmapped** and are never treated as observed `noKO` negatives or complete primary windows.
   - `stop3prime_matched_{KO,noKO}` (`scripts/02_extract_neighbourhoods.py:338-347, 463-485`) requires a **unique** Bakta CDS on the same verified contig sharing the exact 3' stop coordinate (`stop` on `+`, `start` on `-`), identical strand, identical reading frame (`start % 3`), and positive nucleotide overlap (`min(stop_p, stop_b) - max(start_p, start_b) + 1 > 0`). It is evaluated strictly as an explicit sensitivity tier (**Tier 2**), never silently substituted into Tier 1.
2. **Locus Eligibility Determined Prior to Any Family Aggregation (`scripts/02_extract_neighbourhoods.py:495-502`, `scripts/03_analyse_conservation.py:86-225`):**
   - **Tier 1 Primary Gate (`eligible_locus_primary == 1`):** All 4 flanks (`-2, -1, +1, +2`) must be in `{"mapped_KO", "mapped_noKO"}`.
   - **Tier 2 3'-Stop Sensitivity Gate (`eligible_locus_stop3_sens == 1`):** All 4 flanks in `{"mapped_KO", "mapped_noKO", "stop3prime_matched_KO", "stop3prime_matched_noKO"}`.
   - **Tier 3 Partial-Window Sensitivity Gate (`eligible_locus_partial_sens == 1`):** $\geq 1$ flank in `{"mapped_KO", "mapped_noKO"}`.
   - In `scripts/03_analyse_conservation.py:137-205`, primary KO counts (`ko_to_sp_t1`), `n_species_complete_window`, $C_{\text{fam}}^{\text{obs}}$, $R_{\text{fam}}^{\text{obs}}$, and `ko_annotation_opportunity` are computed **exclusively over the identical set of Tier 1 eligible primary loci (`t1_loci`)**.
