These v2 artifacts (executed under Slurm Job ID `53703301` and frozen in `reviews/darkmatter-2026-10-13/round6/snapshots/h2_v2/`) are preserved for auditability and provenance. They are **SUPERSEDED** by v3 to resolve the five critical issues (`C1`–`C5`) identified in `reviews/darkmatter-2026-10-13/round6/h2_review1/review.txt` and `reviews/darkmatter-2026-10-13/round6/ROOT_AUDIT_NOTES.md`:

1. **C1 (Locus Eligibility Before KO Aggregation & Unmapped/Unavailable Callability):** In v2, `03_analyse_conservation.py` aggregated KOs into `ko_by_sp_primary` from any non-truncated flank prior to checking locus completeness (`is_complete`), producing impossible proportions $R_{\text{fam}} > 1.0$ in 4 families and 2 false-positive `QnotK` calls, while counting `source_file_unavailable` and `coordinate_mismatch` flanks as complete/callable negatives.
2. **C2 (Broken `contig_len` Tie-Breaker, Unrefined GTDB-Tk Join, & Non-Converged Logistic Regression):** `01_select_cohort.py` read column 2 (`"refined"`) of `contig_to_bin_refined.tsv.gz` as contig length (`contig_len = 0` for all 534 rows) and read unrefined `02_taxonomy/gtdbtk_out/` bin IDs (leaving 99/100 pilot families as `phylum = "unassigned"`, causing quasi-complete separation and `"converged": false` in logistic regression).
3. **C3 (Unconditioned Positional Null & Invalid Between-Arm Test Statistic):** The v2 permutation null sampled incomplete loci and contig-edge positions and compared the observed between-arm risk difference against a low-prevalence random-position difference distribution (`SD = 0.0681` vs. `0.1336`), creating an artifactual $p = 0.0010$.
4. **C4 (Order/Length-Only Contig Mapping, Silent Missing-Bin Attrition, & `DYNAMIC` Inputs):** Contig mapping relied on FASTA order and length despite 209/293 bins containing duplicate-length contigs, 14/307 bins lacked Bakta files on IBEX, and per-sample/per-bin inputs remained `DYNAMIC`.
5. **C5 (Undocumented 90%-Cluster Representative Basis & Residual Prose):** The 90%-sequence-cluster representative basis of `04_cluster/clusters.tsv` was undocumented, and residual `"candidate novel folds"` and `"operons"` claims remained in `DOSSIER.md`.
