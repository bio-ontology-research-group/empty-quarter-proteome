  Expected correlation under independent counts with common denominator: rho = 0.3017 (95% CI: [0.1789, 0.4194])
  Finding: The negative correlation between dark fraction and RBH fraction is overwhelmingly driven by
           the shared denominator (n_proteins). When n_dark and aai_n_rbh are permuted independently,
           the ratio correlation remains strongly negative (mean null rho = 0.3017).
           Furthermore, the raw count correlation between n_dark and aai_n_rbh is POSITIVE (rho = 0.2419, p = 9.0942e-04).
