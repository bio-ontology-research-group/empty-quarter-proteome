# Proposed HS1–HS4 analysis design — pending executor availability

This is a plan, not an execution record. No new biological results exist.

## Sampling and contrasts
Use correct recorded months: trip1 March2023; trip2 July2023; trip3 February2024; trip4 August2024; trip5 October2025. February is the sole winter visit. October and March remain separate transitional visits. Primary repeated-site comparisons: trip4 versus trip3, and trip2 versus trip3 as a separate geographically restricted contrast. Report trips1/5 separately in sensitivity descriptions, never combine them posthoc to maximise an effect. Confirm site coordinate correspondence and sample-to-trip labels from original provenance. Preserve precise-date source discrepancies if unresolved; monthly categories are robust to the documented within-month discrepancies.

Compare like soil compartments. Aggregate biological replicate measures within site/trip/compartment. Use site-paired inference conditional on these visits, with trip-specific results and explicit limits on temporal generalisation. Only five site IDs have both surface/deep in trips3 and4; depth-by-trip interaction is secondary. Do not infer all551metagenomes are independent seasonal replicates. With only one February visit, no replicated winter seasonal effect is estimable independently of visit/year. Do not fit saturated trip indicators together with season indicators.

## HS1: encoded protein-maintenance capacity and hot-season population composition
Inventory existing domain and orthology annotations with reliable family-specific definitions. SmallHSP/alpha-crystallin, thermosome, DnaK/J/GrpE, GroEL/ES, ClpB and prespecified proteases. Verify ClpB specificity rather than treating all AAAATPases as ClpB. Calculate per-genome copy number and community-weighted capacity on explicit abundance denominators. Separately estimate carrier abundance and copies per represented genome. Include completeness, genome-size and shared-lineage sensitivity. Report effect sizes, site uncertainty and correction across prespecified families/contrasts. Quantify whether signal is between-lineage replacement or among closely related genomes. Gene content is static; abundance-weighted changes are not expression.

## HS2: unknown families linked to protein maintenance
Define unknown set from existing paper thresholds without redefining to favour hits. Test within-contig neighbourhoods with contig-edge eligibility and lineage/genome-matched backgrounds; test conserved recurrence rather than one adjacent pair. Evolutionary co-occurrence requires within-lineage contrasts or phylogenetic adjustment. Correct across all tested families with nonzero empirical p values (b+1)/(B+1). Ecological association must account for carrier/genome abundance; catalogue membership, known divergent HSPs and unknown role remain distinct. Discovery and follow-up evidence are exploratory; no claimed independent validation from reusing the same samples. Candidate structural evidence alone cannot assign a biochemical function.

## HS3: within-species gene/allele changes
Inventory accessible sample mappings, read depth and breadth. Pilot on persistent genomes with adequate unique coverage across matched visits. Measure gene depth relative to genome depth, and allele/haplotype changes if discriminating sites are supported. Include mapping ambiguity and technical repeat checks. Copy-number changes inferred from static species representative annotations are invalid. If data access or coverage fails, retain quantitative eligibility and failure tables; outcome is not testable, not negative. Full eligible cohort follows a successful pilot.

## HS4: depth and environmental attribution
Calculate paired difference-in-differences where both depths are observed at both visits; show actual site-level data and uncertainty. Root-adjacent soils remain a distinct stratum. Inspect visit-specific temperature/moisture/rainfall availability. AnnualMeanTemp cannot test a seasonal temperature mechanism. Public reanalysis may provide exposure proxies, with versions, extraction windows and spatial resolution retained; it does not measure microsite soil conditions. If temperature and dryness cannot be separated, report a seasonal/hot-dry association and leave heat-specific attribution unresolved.
