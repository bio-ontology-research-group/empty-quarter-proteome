# Analysis decisions and audit trail

- Root execution follows the explicit “do it yourself” instruction after the recorded Argon quota failure. No external review has occurred.
- Frozen input scope: eight uniformly annotated families across 5,438 representatives. This is a tractable protein-maintenance panel, not all known stress-response pathways.
- F5Dr1 has all-NaN genome abundance and 100% unmapped reads; excluded from weighted biological calculations, not imputed as a zero-copy community.
- Site-paired tests have only 3, 5 and 6 July/February pairs (surface, deep, root-adjacent) and 9, 13 and 32 August/February pairs. Three-site contrasts are descriptive. Forty of the 48 planned hot-visit tests have sufficient pairs for a p value.
- The initial environmental model is rejected: explicit parsing was needed for July's omitted pressure header, and October site 40 humidity 194 is outside the valid range. Original files remain unchanged. Blank trailing records are ignored. Temperature-only sensitivities retain July despite its missing humidity.
- Coordinate audit identifies site 52 as geographically inconsistent. An exclusion sensitivity is reported, with no guessed coordinate replacement.
- HS2's first 9,999 rotations have a minimum attainable p=0.0001. Because each family contributes at most one locus per genome, its marginal null is exactly a sum of independent Bernoulli variables. An exact convolution computes the same null without Monte Carlo resolution limits. This is a numerical refinement, not a new biological null chosen to improve significance. The initial BH correction covered 3,188 families; after sequence-link correction the accepted universe contains 2,630 families.
- HS2 gene-length matching and one representative per taxonomic genus-label stratum are stricter exploratory sensitivities. An empty GTDB genus label is not a named genus; pooled unresolved strata must not be reported as distinct identified genera or independent evolutionary origins.
- Mixed existing annotations among HS2 candidate members triggered a direct source-sequence audit. Identifier equality alone is insufficient. HS2 candidates remain provisional until that audit is resolved.
- HS3's Thermoplasmatota technical pilot fails the breadth gate. This is lack of usable coverage, not absence of seasonal selection. The bacterial Prauserella_A representative passes. Its matched site 14 root-adjacent pair proceeds to all-read mapping with 128GB allocation. Other matched sites for that representative have much lower abundance (next-highest minimum 0.116% versus 5.846% at site 14), limiting replication. Full-read results remain a single-site feasibility analysis unless additional sites pass quantitative coverage requirements.
- Genomic capacity and read-normalised gene dosage do not measure expression. Allele-frequency shifts can reflect strain replacement; reads are not biological replicates.
- Existing manuscript and other H1–H4/F1–F5 results remain unchanged. Paper inclusion requires the final judgement/options presented to Robert.
- Sequence audit completed: only 100 of 136candidate loci had identical amino-acid sequences under the initial identifier join. All identifier-only HS2 outputs were therefore rejected and retained under `hs2_identifier_only_REJECTED`. The replacement joins all 777,177 Thermoplasmatota proteins to original catalogue loci by exact amino-acid sequence on the same contig, allowing changed gene numbers. Ambiguous or unmatched sequences are excluded from dark-family assignment. All HS2 tests are rerun; no provisional p value is accepted after this failure.

- Replacement sequence linkage completed across 109 original sample proteomes:656,648 unique exact loci,120,069 unmatched,460 ambiguous; 86,642 exact links use a different gene identifier. Original source FASTAs were hashed. The unmatched fraction reflects differences in gene calling and is documented rather than silently reassigned.

- Completed outcomes: no corrected HS1 or HS4 association; five exact-null HS2 candidates, three concordant under length matching, none under sparse genus-label stratification; all-read bacterial HS3 comparison has no large maintenance-locus allele shift and no general summer dosage increase. Root recommends a narrowly framed functional-context addition or supplementary-only reporting. Manuscript unchanged.
