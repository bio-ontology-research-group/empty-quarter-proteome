Interpretation boundaries
Complete gene means Prodigal partial=00, not a closed genome, expression or experimentally established protein function. Genomic neighborhoods and reference-site conservation establish plausibility; they do not measure catalysis, sensing, product formation or stress tolerance. Reference no-hits and non-identical hashes do not prove global novelty or endemism. t-SNE is descriptive; sequence comparisons and full embedding-space statistics are the quantitative evidence. Cross-panel taxonomy and architecture remain potential confounders. No family-specific raw-read remapping or biochemical validation was performed.
