2. TRACK F1: PHOTOLYASE / CRYPTOCHROME DIVERSITY PILOT
------------------------------------------------------
A. Primary Question:
Does the current Empty Quarter MAG catalogue (5,438 ANI95 representatives) contain verifiable, complete photolyase/cryptochrome family (CPF) loci across established subfamilies, and does the sequence evidence establish genuine family membership, coding integrity, and candidate subfamily diversity?

B. Literature Precedent & Domain Architecture:
- Singh et al. 2009 (10.1128/JB.01084-08): Environmental photolyase/cryptochrome subgroup discovery from metagenomes and genomes.
- Xu et al. 2021 (10.1093/molbev/msab191): Functional validation of short photolyase-likes (SPLs) that lack the N-terminal antenna domain and distal Trp triad residue. Short/domain-reduced proteins are established biological variants, NOT automatically truncated artifacts or novel classes.
- Curated reference subfamilies (8 classes):
  1. Class I CPD photolyases (CPD I)
  2. Class II CPD photolyases (CPD II)
  3. Class III CPD photolyases (CPD III) / plant cryptochromes (pCry)
  4. Cryptochrome DASH (CRY-DASH)
  5. DASH-like proteins
  6. Eukaryotic 6-4 photolyases (Eu6-4) / animal cryptochromes (aCry)
  7. FeS-BCP (Iron-sulfur bacterial cryptochromes and photolyases)
  8. Short photolyase-likes (SPL)
- Competing & Outgroup Functions:
  - Spore photoproduct lyase (SplB, radical SAM family; PF04055 / PF00078).
  - Blue-light photoreceptors (BLUF, LOV).
  - Non-photolyase flavoproteins and oxidoreductases sharing Rossmann/FAD folds.

C. Fixed Gates & Criteria:
- Authoritative Profiles:
  - Pfam PF00875 (DNA_photolyase, catalytic FAD-binding domain)
  - Pfam PF03441 (FAD_syn2, cryptochrome FAD-binding domain)
  - Pfam PF04244 (DNA_photolyase_C / N-terminal domain)
- Coding Completeness Gate:
  - Prodigal partial flag must be evaluated: partial=00 denotes complete start and stop codons on the contig.
  - Length distributions recorded: no universal minimum length cutoff that would artificially exclude genuine SPLs (~300-400 aa) while distinguishing genuine short forms from fragmented gene calls.
- Subfamily Assignment Gate:
  - Profile match E-value <= 1e-5; alignment coverage recorded.
  - Reciprocal DIAMOND/BLAST search against curated versioned reference sequences representing all 8 subfamilies and competing outgroups.
- Denominator:
  - Full screen over the complete 5,438 ANI95 representative proteome catalogue (W/06_darkmatter/drep_rep_proteomes/*.faa.gz), explicitly recording total candidate count, carrier MAGs, and taxonomy.

D. Negative Outcome & Stopping Conditions:
- FAIL: Zero complete candidate loci match CPF profiles at standard thresholds, or all matches are radical SAM SplB / non-photolyase flavoprotein false positives.
- INCONCLUSIVE: Hits are exclusively edge-truncated fragments or low-confidence profile matches without conserved FAD-binding residues.
- PASS: Identified candidate loci have intact coding sequences (partial=00), defensible CPF domain profiles, clear subfamily discrimination, and include well-resolved taxonomic and ecological distributions.

3. TRACK F2: COMPATIBLE-SOLUTE PHOSPHATASE PILOT
------------------------------------------------
A. Primary Question:
Do historical 306 dark-orthogroup compatible-solute phosphatase predictions—specifically the two named lead IDs in ROOT_ASSESSMENT—remap to intact loci in the current catalogue, and does sequence/profile evidence support specific mannosyl-3-phosphoglycerate phosphatase (MpgP, EC 3.1.3.70) or trehalose-6-phosphate phosphatase (TPP, EC 3.1.3.69/12) activity over competing HAD-superfamily functions?

B. Lead Identifiers from ROOT_ASSESSMENT:
- Lead 1: 1PRr2__self_SemiBin_81::1PRr2_1391604_4
  Historical annotation: EC 3.1.3.70 (MpgP), 127 members across 85 samples.
- Lead 2: S52PRr1__single_SemiBin_53::S52PRr1_477345_9
  Historical annotation: EC 3.1.3.70 (MpgP), 37 members across 34 samples, attributed to Thermoplasmatota SW-10-69-26.

C. Fixed Gates & Analysis Hierarchy:
1. Current-Catalogue Remapping & Integrity:
   - Remap historical protein IDs to current 14,426 refined bins and 5,438 ANI95 representatives via contig_to_bin_refined.tsv.gz and drep Wdb/Cdb.
   - Evaluate Prodigal coding completeness (partial=00), internal stop codons, and contig length.
2. Competing HAD-Superfamily Discrimination:
   - MpgP and TPP belong to the HAD (haloacid dehalogenase) superfamily. Generic HAD fold similarity is insufficient to assign substrate specificity.
   - Reference comparator sets:
     * Curated MpgP (EC 3.1.3.70) experimentally characterized sequences (e.g. Thermus thermophilus, Pyrococcus horikoshii, Deinococcus radiodurans).
     * Curated TPP (OtsB, EC 3.1.3.12 / 3.1.3.69) characterized sequences (e.g. E. coli, Mycobacterium, Streptomyces).
     * Competing HAD enzymes: phosphoglycolate phosphatase (PGP, EC 3.1.3.18), sucrose-6-phosphatase (SPP, EC 3.1.3.24), histidinol-phosphatase (EC 3.1.3.15), beta-phosphoglucomutase (EC 5.4.2.6), phosphopentomutase (EC 5.4.2.7), and non-specific sugar phosphatases.
   - Profile match: TIGR01416 (mpgP), PF00702 (HAD_hydrolase), and custom HMMs. Evaluate relative bit score and alignment depth.
   - Conserved catalytic motifs: Verify HAD signature motifs (Motif I: DXDX(T/V), Motif II: [S/T], Motif III: K, Motif IV: [G/E]DXXXD).
3. Genomic Context & Pathway Co-occurrence:
   - Extract +/- 5 flanking genes from the verified contig assembly and Bakta/eggNOG annotations.
   - Test for co-localization or co-occurrence with compatible-solute biosynthetic enzymes:
     * MpgP pathway: mannosyl-3-phosphoglycerate synthase (MpgS, EC 2.4.1.217), GDP-mannose pyrophosphorylase / mannose-1-phosphate guanylyltransferase.
     * Trehalose pathway: trehalose-6-phosphate synthase (OtsA, EC 2.4.1.15).
   - Test whether apparent pathway absence in a MAG reflects genuine biological independence versus contig edge truncation or genome incompleteness (CheckM2).
4. Broader Historical Census Remapping:
   - Assess how many of the top 306 historical osmolyte-phosphatase families map into current representative bins, their dark vs annotated status, and their dominant EC annotations.

D. Negative Outcome & Stopping Conditions:
- FAIL: Historical leads do not exist in current assemblies, are pseudogenes/frameshifted fragments, or align with higher similarity to generic housekeeping HAD phosphatases (e.g. PGP, sugar phosphatase) than to MpgP/TPP, with zero compatible-solute context.
- INCONCLUSIVE: Leads are genuine HAD-family phosphatases but lack active-site pocket specificity or conserved pathway context to discriminate MpgP from competing substrates.
- PASS: Candidate loci are complete (partial=00), remap to current catalogue, exhibit clear sequence/profile alignment preference for MpgP or TPP over competing HAD enzymes, and/or demonstrate conserved genomic association with osmolyte biosynthetic partners.
