3. TRACK F3: RHODOPSIN & HELIORHODOPSIN DIVERSITY PILOT
-------------------------------------------------------
A. Primary Scientific Questions:
1. Does the current 5,438 ANI95 representative catalogue contain verified, complete
   rhodopsin loci belonging to established Type-1 microbial rhodopsin subfamilies
   or Type-3 heliorhodopsins (HeRs)?
2. Do EQ rhodopsins exhibit genuine reference-relative sequence divergence, and do
   they partition into sensory vs energy-harvesting clades based on conserved motifs
   and recurrent genomic contexts (e.g. histidine kinases, two-component systems)?
3. Can terrestrial actinobacterial rhodopsins (e.g. in Geodermatophilaceae) be
   distinguished from established terrestrial groups (Tarlachkov et al. 2024/2025)?

B. Retained Literature Precedent & Domain Architectures:
- Pushkarev et al. 2018 (Nature, 10.1038/s41586-018-0225-9): Discovery of
  heliorhodopsins (HeRs, Type-3) from Lake Kinneret fosmid 48C12 (GenBank MF737519.1 -> AVZ43932.1).
  HeRs adopt an inverted membrane orientation with conserved retinal-binding Lys in TM7.
- Bulzu et al. 2021 (mSphere, 10.1128/msphere.00661-21): Evidence that monoderm HeRs
  function as photosensory receptors coupled to two-component histidine kinases.
- Bulzu et al. 2022 (mSystems, 10.1128/msystems.00405-22): Global rhodopsin survey
  defining Alt-rhodopsins, diverse Type-1 and Type-3 clades.
- Tarlachkov et al. 2024/2025 (J Photochem Photobiol / bioRxiv, 10.1016/j.jphotobiol.2024.113083):
  Rhodopsins in arid Geodermatophilaceae (terrestrial actinobacteria). Desert occurrence
  alone is NOT novel; reference-relative divergence and context are required.

C. Authoritative Profiles & Competing Family Discrimination:
- Target Family 1: Microbial Type-1 Rhodopsin
  * Pfam: PF01036 (Bac_rhodopsin, Bacteriorhodopsin-like protein domain).
  * InterPro: IPR001425.
  * Active site hallmark: Retinal-binding Lysine in TM7 forming protonated Schiff base.
- Target Family 2: Heliorhodopsin (Type-3)
  * Pfam: PF18761 (Heliorhodopsin).
  * NCBIFAM: NF038020 (heliorhodopsin HeR).
  * InterPro: IPR041113.
  * Structural hallmark: Inverted 7TM topology, retinal-binding Lysine in TM7.
- Competing Outgroups & Negative Controls:
  * Pfam PF00001 (7tm_1, Eukaryotic rhodopsin-like GPCR family).
  * Pfam PF00002 (7tm_2, Secretin-like GPCR family).
  * Rule: Avoid treating all 7-transmembrane proteins as rhodopsins. A candidate must
    match PF01036 or PF18761/NF038020, possess 7 predicted transmembrane helices,
    and retain the retinal-binding Lysine residue.

D. Predetermined QC Gates & Classification:
- Gate F3-1 (Coding Completeness): Prodigal partial=00 (complete start and stop codons).
- Gate F3-2 (Domain Match): HMMER domtblout independent E-value <= 1e-5 to PF01036
  or PF18761/NF038020; domain coverage >= 70% of profile HMM.
- Gate F3-3 (Key Residue Conservation): Alignment to profile must confirm presence of
  the conserved retinal-binding Lysine in the 7th transmembrane helix.
- Gate F3-4 (Competing Discrimination): Bit score to target profile must exceed bit score
  to GPCR/non-rhodopsin controls (PF00001, PF00002) by >= 20 bits.
- Gate F3-5 (Genomic Context): Inspect flanking +/- 5 genes for phototrophic or sensory
  markers (CRT operon / carotenoid genes, two-component histidine kinases, response regulators).

4. TRACK F4: ECTOINE SYNTHASE (EctC) & PATHWAY CONTEXT PILOT
-----------------------------------------------------------
A. Primary Scientific Questions:
1. Does the current catalogue encode genuine EctC ectoine synthases (EC 4.2.1.108),
   and do candidate loci represent complete operons (ectABC / ectABCD) versus orphan
   EctC-like genes (Czech et al. 2019)?
2. Are candidate EctC loci distinguishable from competing cupin superfamily members?
3. What is the true genomic context and taxonomic distribution across the 5,438 MAGs?

B. Retained Literature Precedent & Domain Architecture:
- Czech et al. 2019 (Sci Rep, 10.1038/s41598-018-36247-w): Structural and biochemical
  characterisation of EctC enzymes and orphan EctC-like proteins. Establishes that EctC
  belongs to the cupin superfamily with a conserved 2-His-1-carboxylate iron-binding triad
  (Glu and two His residues) within cupin motifs.
- Disambiguation from competing cupins: Generic cupins (PF00190, PF07883, Pirin PF02678)
  perform diverse reactions (dioxygenases, epimerases, isomerases, oxalate oxidases).
  Treating all cupins as EctC is invalid.

C. Authoritative Profiles & Context Markers:
- Target Family: Ectoine Synthase (EctC, EC 4.2.1.108)
  * Pfam: PF06339 (Ectoine_synth, Ectoine synthase family).
  * NCBIFAM: NF009806 (ectoine synthase).
  * InterPro: IPR010462.
  * CDD: cd06978 (L-ectoine synthase, cupin domain).
- Competing Cupin Controls (Negative Controls):
  * Pfam PF00190 (Cupin_1, generic cupin domain).
  * Pfam PF07883 (Cupin_2, cupin domain 2).
  * Pfam PF02678 (Pirin, iron-binding pirin cupin family).
- Ectoine Biosynthetic Pathway Context Markers:
  * EctA: L-2,4-diaminobutyrate acetyltransferase (EC 2.3.1.178) -> NCBIFAM TIGR02406.
  * EctB: diaminobutyrate--2-oxoglutarate transaminase (EC 2.6.1.76) -> NCBIFAM TIGR02407.
  * EctD: ectoine hydroxylase (EC 1.14.11.55) -> NCBIFAM TIGR02408.

D. Predetermined QC Gates & Classification:
- Gate F4-1 (Coding Completeness): Prodigal partial=00.
- Gate F4-2 (EctC Specificity): Bit score to PF06339 / NF009806 must exceed bit score to
  generic Cupin_1 (PF00190) and Pirin (PF02678) by >= 25 bits; E-value <= 1e-10.
- Gate F4-3 (Catalytic Core Integrity): Czech et al. 2019 iron-binding triad (2-His-1-carboxylate)
  must be conserved in multiple sequence alignment against Paenibacillus lautus EctC (YP_003245677.1).
- Gate F4-4 (Context Classification):
  * Class A: Intact operon (EctA, EctB, EctC co-localized within 5 kb).
  * Class B: Unlinked pathway (EctC present, EctA/B present elsewhere in same MAG).
  * Class C: Orphan EctC (EctC present, no EctA/B detected in MAG; verify MAG completeness).
- Gate F4-5 (Environmental Caution): Elemental XRF ranks do not represent porewater salinity;
  ecological models must account for MAG taxonomy, site, and expedition.

5. TRACK F5: CARBONIC ANHYDRASE (Beta-CA and Gamma-CA) PILOT
------------------------------------------------------------
A. Primary Scientific Questions:
1. Does the current catalogue encode distinct classes of carbonic anhydrases (EC 4.2.1.1),
   specifically Beta-CA and Gamma-CA?
2. Are Gamma-CA candidates distinguishable from competing left-handed parallel beta-helix
   (LbH) enzymes (hexapeptide repeat transferases, LpxA-like acetyltransferases)?
3. Which class(es) demonstrate verified family membership, non-redundant sequence diversity,
   and conserved catalytic metal-coordination sites?

B. Retained Literature Precedent & Domain Architectures:
- Pairoh et al. 2025 (Sci Rep, 10.1038/s41598-025-24713-1): Metagenomic discovery and
  biochemical validation of thermophilic Gamma-CAs (TtCA YEH58108.1, ToCA YEH58110.1, CrCA YEH58109.1).
  Validates Gamma-CA only; does not validate Beta-CA or establish that fold similarity implies activity.
- Structural distinction between classes:
  * Beta-CA: Alpha/beta fold, coordinated by Cys-His-Cys triad (e.g. Cys42, His98, Cys101).
  * Gamma-CA: Trimeric left-handed parallel beta-helix (LbH) fold, catalytic zinc coordinated
    by three Histidines located at monomer interfaces (e.g. His81, His117, His122 in Cam).
- Non-CA LbH Competing Enzymes:
  * Hexapeptide repeat acetyltransferases (LpxA, DapD, etc.) share the identical LbH fold
    (PF00132 Hexapep, PF00583 Acetyltransf_1) but lack the catalytic zinc-binding Histidines
    and have zero carbonic anhydrase activity. Avoid treating all LbH structures as active CAs!
- Carboxysome CAs Kept Strictly Separate:
  * CsoSCA / CcmM (TIGR02701, IPR017156) represent specialized shell-associated enzymes
    and must NOT be conflated with genuine Gamma-CA.

C. Authoritative Profiles & Competing Discrimination:
- Target Family 1: Gamma-Carbonic Anhydrase (γ-CA)
  * NCBIFAM: NF050158 (gamma carbonic anhydrase, CarbAnhydGPos).
  * NCBIFAM: NF040597 (Carbonic anhydrase, gamma-class, carb_anhyd).
  * InterPro: IPR052265 (Gamma-class Carbonic Anhydrase), IPR047223 (CA_gamma_LbH).
  * CDD: cd00710 (LbH_gamma_CA).
- Target Family 2: Beta-Carbonic Anhydrase (β-CA)
  * Pfam: PF00484 (Pro_CA, Carbonic anhydrase, prokaryotic beta-class).
  * InterPro: IPR001788 (Carbonic anhydrase, beta-class).
- Separate Class: Carboxysome Carbonic Anhydrase
  * NCBIFAM: TIGR02701 (carboxysome shell carbonic anhydrase CsoSCA).
  * InterPro: IPR017156 (Carboxysome assembly protein CcmM).
- Competing Outgroups & Negative Controls:
  * Pfam PF00194 (Carb_anhydrase, Eukaryotic Alpha-CA family).
  * Pfam PF00132 (Hexapep, bacterial transferase hexapeptide repeat domain).
  * Pfam PF00583 (Acetyltransf_1, GNAT acetyltransferase family).
  * SSF51161 / IPR011004 (Trimeric LpxA-like superfamily).

D. Predetermined QC Gates & Classification:
- Separate Evaluation Mandate: Beta-CA and Gamma-CA must be evaluated separately;
  no pooling into a single novelty endpoint.
- Gate F5-1 (Coding Completeness): Prodigal partial=00.
- Gate F5-2 (Class-Specific Match):
  * For Gamma-CA: Independent E-value <= 1e-7 to NF050158 / NF040597 / cd00710;
    bit score must exceed PF00132 (Hexapep) and PF00583 (Acetyltransf_1) by >= 20 bits.
  * For Beta-CA: Independent E-value <= 1e-7 to PF00484; domain coverage >= 70%.
- Gate F5-3 (Active-Site Metal Coordination):
  * For Gamma-CA: Conservation of the three catalytic Histidine residues coordinating zinc.
  * For Beta-CA: Conservation of the catalytic Cys-His-Cys triad.
- Gate F5-4 (No Biochemical Overstatement): Sequence identification establishes genetic
  capacity; NO claims of thermostability, extremophilic adaptation, or kinetic rate
  follow from sequence origin. Direct assays are required for applied claims.
