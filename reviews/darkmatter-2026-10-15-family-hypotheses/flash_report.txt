1.3 Core Epistemological and Methodological Principles
To avoid common pitfalls in computational metagenomics, all candidate
hypotheses enforce the following standards:
1. Sequence Diversity != Novel Enzymatic Function: As established by the
   catalogue-wide decode audit (RQ/A5/result.md), sequence-dark proteins
   predominantly represent divergent folds or sequences performing conventional,
   canonical chemistry (oxidoreductases, transferases, hydrolases), rather than
   novel reaction chemistry. No candidate hypothesis posits unverified "exotic"
   enzymology without direct structural or active-site evidence.
2. Full-Dimensional ProstT5 Embedding Novelty: In accordance with Wu et al.
   (2025) and Heinzinger et al. (2024), structural embeddings from protein
   language models must be evaluated quantitatively in full-dimensional vector
   space (1024-dimensional per-protein or pooled embeddings) using rigorous
   nearest-neighbour cosine and Euclidean distance distributions against
   curated reference sets. Low-dimensional visualizations (t-SNE, UMAP) are
   strictly descriptive and cannot serve as inferential proof of novelty.
3. Pre-Training Exposure Caveat: Absence of a sequence from current public
   databases does NOT prove that a foundation protein language model (ProstT5,
   ESM) was never exposed to homologous sequences during self-supervised
   pre-training. Any embedding-derived distance must be paired with orthogonal
   evidence (coordinate modeling, MSAs, catalytic site geometry).
4. Environmental Data and Confounding: Geochemical covariates (XRF total
   elements) originate from the verified laboratory matrix, but absolute units
   remain unresolved (manuscript/main.tex line 500; notes/CORRECTIONS.md).
   All environmental associations must use non-parametric rank metrics
   (Spearman, partial Spearman controlling for ranked latitude/longitude) and
   site-blocked permutation tests to account for the spatial transect (59 sites)
   and temporal sampling (5 expeditions over 31 months).
5. Terrestrial Provenance != Intrinsic Thermostability: Isolation from a desert
   soil does not warrant an automatic claim of hyperthermostability or novel
   biocatalytic efficiency. Every hypothesis separates ecological occurrence
   from biophysical properties.

- Biological Question:
  Does the sequence-dark and low-homology proteome of Rub' al-Khali soil MAGs

- Target Family & Orthogonal Definition:
  Photolyase/cryptochrome superfamily (deoxyribodipyrimidine photolyase,
  EC 4.1.99.3; cryptochrome DASH, (6-4) photolyase, or FeS-BCP clades).
  Orthogonal definition requires:
  (a) Presence of the FAD-binding alpha/beta domain fold (Pfam PF00875 /
      PF04244 / PF03441; CATH 3.40.50.360);
  (b) Query-normalised TM-score >= 0.50 against experimental photolyase
      crystal structures (e.g. PDB 1TEZ, 2BPR, 3CVU) in ESMFold coordinates;
  (c) Conservation of the characteristic active-site electron-transfer aromatic
      triad (three Trp/Tyr residues mediating flavin photoreduction);
  (d) Absence of canonical EggNOG KO assignment (K01669) in the sequence search.

- Feasibility Basis & Exact Existing Evidence:
  1. manuscript/main.tex (lines 1181-1185): Canonical photolyase (K01669) is one
     of the sharpest vertical ecological markers in the transect: 24.3% in
     surface mapped community vs 12.1% in subsurface across 44 paired sites
     (Wilcoxon signed-rank p < 1e-6), replicating independently within all three
     major phyla with sufficient paired coverage (Actinomycetota, Pseudomonadota,
     Bacteroidota), while being null within-species (p > 0.17).
  2. results/uv_survival/UV.md & compartment.tsv: Surface MAGs carry 22.4%
     photolyase carriage vs 13.1% in Deep (MWU p = 1.12e-14), whereas NHEJ
     (desiccation/dormancy repair) reverses (Deep 81.1% > Surface 73.0%),
     proving a clean, mechanistically decoupled light niche.
  3. Singh et al. (2009) J Bacteriol (DOI 10.1128/JB.01084-08; PMC2612456):
     Demonstrated that high-UV environmental metagenomes harbor deep-branching,
     unannotated photolyase/cryptochrome subfamilies that evade standard BLAST/HMM
     cutoffs but preserve core functional folds.
  4. manuscript/main.tex (lines 320-337): 2,838 dark models have ESMFold coordinates
     with pLDDT >= 0.70; 1,031 are low-qTM dual-reference candidates available

    flavin-binding cavity and the conserved Trp/Tyr electron-transfer triad.
  - Read-recruitment mapping (TPM) across the 44 paired surface/subsurface sites

- Primary Effect & Quantitative Prediction:
  Candidate dark photolyase-fold families will exhibit a statistically significant

  2. manuscript/main.tex (lines 311-313): Rhodopsin markers are completely absent

  3. Pushkarev et al. (2018) Nature (DOI 10.1038/s41586-018-0225-9) & Bulzu

HYPOTHESIS 4 (RANK 4)
Alpha/Beta Hydrolase Superfamily Diversity and Quantitative ProstT5 Embedding
Novelty in Benign Soil Metabolic Pathways
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Biological Question:
  Can full-dimensional ProstT5 bilingual protein language model embeddings
  quantitatively identify sequence-divergent alpha/beta hydrolases within the
  genome-resolved dark proteome, and do these candidates preserve the canonical
  catalytic triad (Ser-His-Asp/Glu) and oxyanion hole required for benign soil
  ester/thioester turnover while exhibiting robust metric distance from known
  reference enzymes?

- Target Family & Orthogonal Definition:
  Alpha/beta hydrolase fold superfamily (Pfam PF00561, PF12695; CATH 3.40.50.1820;
  e.g. palmitoyl-[ACP] thioesterase EC 3.1.2.14, carboxylesterases EC 3.1.1.1,
  haloalkane dehalogenases EC 3.8.1.5).
  Orthogonal definition requires:
  (a) Identification of the core alpha/beta hydrolase fold (central beta-sheet
      flanked by alpha-helices with canonical strand order);
  (b) Preservation of the nucleophilic elbow motif (Sm-X-Nu-X-Sm, typically
      Gly-X-Ser-X-Gly) and intact Ser-His-Asp catalytic triad geometry;
  (c) Nearest-neighbour distance in full 1024-dimensional ProstT5 embedding
      space that places the candidate within the alpha/beta hydrolase manifold
      while maintaining high sequence divergence (<25% amino acid identity).

- Feasibility Basis & Exact Existing Evidence:
  1. RQ/A5/result.md: Hydrolases (EC 3.x) represent 1,340 of the 5,418 confident
     specific dark decodes (24.7%). Palmitoyl-[ACP] thioesterase (EC 3.1.2.14,
     PWY-5994: fatty acid biosynthesis termination) is among the single most
     frequent confident decodes:
     - V30PRr1…825193_17: EC 3.1.2.14, C_confidence = 0.776.
     - V24Dr1…1034307_7: EC 3.1.2.14, C_confidence = 0.730.
  2. results/darkmatter_ecology/indicators/partial_salinity_proxy_top.tsv:
     Family 24Dr2__self_SemiBin_62::24Dr2_840497_13 (EC 3.1.2.14, RXN-9548)
     is present in 203 samples, dN/dS = 0.3627, and shows strong partial
     correlation with salinity (partial r = -0.413, FDR q = 6.44e-08).
  3. results/darkmatter_ecology/indicators/partial_aridity_index_top.tsv:
     Family 25PRr1__self_SemiBin_1258::25PRr1_262260_5 (EC 3.1.2.14) is present
     in 97 samples, dN/dS = 0.3912, partial r = -0.279 (FDR q = 1.88e-06).
  4. Wu et al. (2025) Nat Commun (PMC12228687 / DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-61599-z):
     Demonstrated that ProstT5 embeddings in full-dimensional vector space
     enable representation tree discovery of environmental alpha/beta hydrolases
     from metagenomic data, bridging wide sequence gaps to recover robust enzymes.
  5. Heinzinger et al. (2024) NAR Genom Bioinform (PMC11616678): Validated
     ProstT5 bilingual language modeling for structure extraction directly from
     sequence.

- Data Missing & Analytical Requirements:
  - Extraction of 1024-dimensional sequence embeddings from ProstT5 for the
    top confident dark hydrolase candidates and a curated reference set of
    PDB alpha/beta hydrolases.
  - Computation of full-dimensional Euclidean and cosine distance distributions
    to quantify structural proximity without dimensionality reduction artifacts.
  - Coordinate modeling (ESMFold) to verify the geometric spatial configuration
    of the catalytic triad (nucleophile-acid-base distances <= 3.5 Angstroms).
  - Explicit documentation of language model pre-training exposure limits.

- Primary Effect & Quantitative Prediction:
  Candidate dark families will fall within the quantitative distribution of
  known alpha/beta hydrolases in full-dimensional ProstT5 embedding space
  (nearest-neighbour cosine similarity > 0.70 vs < 0.40 for non-hydrolase
  controls), while maintaining pairwise sequence identity < 25% against all
  PDB references. Predicted coordinates will show root-mean-square deviation
  (RMSD) < 2.5 Angstroms across the catalytic core with intact triad geometry.

- Appropriate Controls & Calibration:
  1. Negative Structural Controls: Non-hydrolase alpha/beta proteins (e.g.
     flavodoxin-like folds, Rossmann folds).
  2. Downsampled Reference Arm: Matched comparison against downsampled GEM
     and SMAG soil references following the H3 protocol to control for database
     growth effects.
  3. Active-Site Scrambled Null: Shuffling catalytic residue positions to verify
     that triad convergence is not an artifact of model smoothing.
  4. Frame-Shift Selection Null: dN/dS comparison against the +1 shifted frame
     to confirm protein-coding constraint.

- Rejection / Falsification Outcome:
  Rejected if:
  (a) Nearest-neighbour distances in full-dimensional ProstT5 space are
      statistically indistinguishable from random sequences or non-hydrolase folds; OR
  (b) Coordinate models fail to reconstruct the nucleophilic elbow or displace
      catalytic triad residues beyond hydrogen-bonding distance (> 4.0 Angstroms); OR
  (c) Purifying selection is absent (dN/dS >= 1.0).
