1. EXECUTIVE ASSESSMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
`DESIGN.txt` appropriately freezes H1–H4 and the completed F1–F5 screening state,
drops F2, holds F5 at its stop rule, and targets the three genuine analytical
gaps identified in `RESULTS_FOR_AUTHOR.txt` and `ROOT_F345_JUDGEMENT.txt`:
  (1) whether F3 site-conserving heliorhodopsin cluster diversity (`13.98` vs
      `5.00` SMAG / `4.79` GEM external 50%-clusters at `n = 35`) is an artefact
      of host taxonomy (`Patescibacteriota` / `Actinomycetota`) and N/C-terminal
      length variation;
  (2) how much of the F1 surface-vs-deep CPF enrichment (`+8.47` percentage
      points, Holm `p = 0.00196`) reflects between-lineage community turnover vs
      within-lineage carrier shifts; and
  (3) how many F4 residue-conserving (`W21/E57/Y84/H92`) `EctC` genes sit in
      complete (`partial=00`) same-strand `EctA`+`EctB` neighborhoods and how
      that strict carrier fraction varies across paired soils.

However, inspection of the underlying audited tables (`f3_site_conserving/`,
`f345_final_audit/audit.json`, and `ecology/f1_ecology_root_v3.py`) reveals six
critical design flaws that would bias or invalidate the follow-up inferences if
not corrected prior to execution. Section 2 details these flaws, Section 3
specifies bounded feasible stronger tests using existing IBEX data, Section 4
separates required inference controls from optional scope expansion, and
Section 5 assesses plausible manuscript value.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CRITICAL DESIGN FLAWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Flaw 1 (F3 — Small-Stratum Degeneracy & Upstream Panel-Sampling Bias):
  In `root_audit/f3_site_conserving/Helio/metadata.tsv`, the `45` site-conserving
  (`K241`) EQ heliorhodopsins (`44` unique sequences; `20` excluded, `19/20` in
  `Patescibacteriota`) span only five phyla: `Patescibacteriota` (`24` genes,
  `23` unique), `Actinomycetota` (`16`), `Chloroflexota` (`2`), `Halobacteriota`
  (`2`), and `Deinococcota` (`1`). Comparator totals across all phyla are only
  `35` SMAG (`35` unique) and `73` GEM (`71` unique), drawn from the sampled H3
  protein pools (`4.78M` SMAG and `4.82M` GEM proteins vs `20.45M` EQ proteins),
  not the full published SMAG/GEM catalogues. Conditioning 3-way equal-size
  rarefaction simultaneously on `EQ ∩ SMAG ∩ GEM` within each phylum will reduce
  per-phylum sample sizes `n_p` to `0` or `< 5` whenever SMAG or GEM lacks
  sufficient `Patescibacteriota` or `Actinomycetota` hits, and still reflects
  upstream MAG completeness/binning differences for CPR (`Patescibacteriota`).

Flaw 2 (F3 — Taxonomically Unannotated Curated Reference Panel in "Reference-
  External" Cluster Metrics):
  `DESIGN.txt` retains the fixed joint 50%/80% MMseqs2 cluster partition and
  measures clusters outside `curated_reference` (`n = 25` site-conserving refs,
  all currently labelled `phylum = Unknown` in `Helio/metadata.tsv`). If the `25`
  curated references lack `Patescibacteriota` representatives, any
  `Patescibacteriota` heliorhodopsin from EQ, SMAG, or GEM is automatically
  "reference-external." Evaluating reference-external clusters without
  annotating reference taxonomy and without measuring direct EQ-to-(SMAG ∪ GEM)
  within-phylum sequence/embedding distances conflates reference-database
  taxonomic gaps with desert-specific sequence divergence.

Flaw 3 (F3 — Length-Coverage (`-c 0.80`) Splitting from Terminal Extensions vs.
  7TM Core Divergence):
  The frozen joint MMseqs2 clustering enforces `>= 80%` bidirectional sequence
  coverage (`-c 0.80`). In `Helio/metadata.tsv`, EQ `Actinomycetota` candidates
  are `243–280 aa`, whereas EQ `Patescibacteriota` candidates reach `349–352 aa`
  and GEM reaches `406 aa`. Because `255 / 340 = 0.75 < 0.80`, full-length
  bidirectional coverage splits sequences with N/C-terminal extensions into
  separate clusters even when their 7TM core domains exceed 50% identity.
  Relying solely on the frozen full-length cluster partition cannot distinguish
  core 7TM sequence divergence from terminal extension length variation.

Flaw 4 (F1 & F4 — Non-Additivity of Medians, Unshared Taxonomic Support, and
  Pooled "Unknown" Genera in Lineage Decomposition):
  `DESIGN.txt` specifies decomposing paired surface/deep carrier abundance into
  between-lineage composition and within-lineage carrier-fraction components at
  phylum and genus ranks using replicate medians and site median contrasts:
  (a) Non-additivity & replicate renormalization: Taking taxon-by-taxon medians
      across replicate samples within `(site, trip, compartment)` violates
      `sum_g w_g = 1`, and site medians of components do not sum to the site
      median of total `Delta_Y`.
  (b) Unshared support at Genus rank: When a genus is detected in Surface
      (`w_{g,S} > 0`) but absent in Deep (`w_{g,D} = 0`) or vice versa, its
      within-lineage carrier fraction `p_g` is undefined in the absent
      compartment. Restricting only to common-support taxa (`w_{g,S} > 0` and
      `w_{g,D} > 0`) omits the compartment-exclusive lineage turnover residual
      `R`, biasing the proportion of total `Delta_Y` explained.
  (c) Ecological fallacy of pooling `genus = Unknown`: Many EQ MAGs across
      divergent phyla/orders/families have `genus = Unknown`. Pooling them into
      one global `"Unknown"` genus falsely reclassifies cross-phylum/cross-family
      compositional turnover as a "within-genus" shift.
  (d) Static species-representative resolution limit: Because CoverM maps reads
      to `5,438` static ANI95 species representatives with binary trait calls
      (`0` or `1`), any genus represented by a single MAG has `p_{g,S} == p_{g,D}`
      (`W_g = 0` identically), and intra-species strain turnover is unobservable.

Flaw 5 (F4 — Contig-Edge Truncation Confounding of Strict Neighborhoods):
  In the completed audit (`ROOT_F345_JUDGEMENT.txt`), `623/940` complete EQ
  `EctC` candidates satisfied `WEYH` + same-strand `EctA`+`EctB` within 5 kb,
  where `EctA`/`EctB` only required `>= 50%` profile coverage (`partial=00` not
  enforced). Tightening the gate to require `partial=00` for both `EctA` and
  `EctB` will drop loci where `EctA` or `EctB` is truncated at a contig edge. If
  contig-boundary-truncated loci (`<= 5 kb` from contig end) are lumped with
  internal non-operonic `EctC` genes, differences in MAG assembly contiguity
  between surface and deep communities can artefactually skew the paired soil
  contrast.

Flaw 6 (F1/F4 Inference — Redundant Re-Testing of Known F1 Contrast & Unstated
  `K01669` Overlap):
  `DESIGN.txt` proposes *"Holm correction across the two new overall ecology
  contrasts."* However, the F1 overall `cpf_complete` surface-vs-deep contrast
  (`+8.47 pp`, Wilcoxon `p = 2.34e-4`, Holm `p = 0.00196`) was already tested in
  `ecology/results_root_v3`, where it was also shown that `cpf_complete_exclusive`
  (complete CPF carriers lacking prior `K01669` annotation) is null (`+0.0045 pp`,
  `p = 0.533`). Only the F4 soil compartment contrast is a new overall test; F1
  follow-up is a decomposition of an already-established contrast that overlaps
  the manuscript's `K01669` baseline.

3. BOUNDED FEASIBLE STRONGER TESTS (IBEX SLURM, EXISTING DATA ONLY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Test A — F3 Taxonomy- and Architecture-Controlled Core vs. Full-Length Test:
  1. Taxonomic Closure: Join GTDB phylum/genus taxonomy for all SMAG (`35`) and
     GEM (`73`) site-conserving heliorhodopsins from original publication tables,
     and assign NCBI/GTDB phylum for all `25` site-conserving `curated_reference`
     sequences.
  2. Within-Phylum Pairwise & 3-Way Comparisons: For each shared phylum (`p`)
     with `>= 5` unique site-conserving sequences in at least two sources
     (specifically `Actinomycetota` and `Patescibacteriota`), report:
     - 3-way equal-size rarefaction where `min(n_EQ, n_SMAG, n_GEM) >= 5`, AND
       pairwise equal-size rarefaction (`EQ vs GEM`, `EQ vs SMAG`) whenever one
       comparator has `< 5` sequences;
     - Direct reference-free environmental distance: each unique EQ sequence's
       nearest neighbor in `SMAG ∪ GEM` within the same phylum (both global
       pairwise percent identity and 1024-d ProstT5 cosine distance), compared
       against leave-one-MAG-out within-source distances.
  3. Core-Domain vs. Full-Length Clustering Check: Run MMseqs2 50% and 80%
     clustering (`-c 0.80 --cov-mode 0`) on the trimmed HMM-envelope/match core
     domains of the exact same `44` EQ, `35` SMAG, `71` GEM, and `25` reference
     unique sequences alongside the frozen full-length partition. Quantify how
     much of the `13.98` vs `5.00`/`4.79` gap is removed by (i) trimming N/C
     extensions and (ii) conditioning on `Patescibacteriota` vs `Actinomycetota`.

Test B — F1 & F4 Exact Three-Term Kitagawa Decomposition with Taxonomic Fallback:
  1. Replicate Aggregation: Within each `(site, trip, compartment)` cell,
     average replicate sample taxon relative abundances (or renormalize cell
     medians so `sum_g w_g = 1.0` exactly) before forming paired within-trip
     differences `Delta_Y_{s,tr} = Y_{s,tr,S} - Y_{s,tr,D}`.
  2. Exact Three-Term Partition per Paired Visit `(s, tr)`:
     - `B_{s,tr}` (common-support between-lineage composition):
       `sum_{g in G_SD} (w_{g,S} - w_{g,D}) * ((p_{g,S} + p_{g,D}) / 2)`
     - `W_{s,tr}` (common-support within-lineage carrier shift):
       `sum_{g in G_SD} ((w_{g,S} + w_{g,D}) / 2) * (p_{g,S} - p_{g,D})`
     - `R_{s,tr}` (unshared-support lineage turnover):
       `sum_{g in G_S \ G_D} w_{g,S} * p_{g,S} - sum_{g in G_D \ G_S} w_{g,D} * p_{g,D}`
     Verify in code that `B_{s,tr} + W_{s,tr} + R_{s,tr} == Delta_Y_{s,tr}` to
     floating-point precision (`< 1e-12`) for every paired visit.
  3. Unknown-Genus Handling: Assign every MAG with `genus == "Unknown"` to its
     finest resolved higher-rank taxon string
     (`<phylum>;<class>;<order>;<family>;g__unclassified`), never a single
     cross-phylum `"Unknown"` bucket. Report the fraction of community abundance
     in common-support genera, multi-MAG genera (where `W_g` can be non-zero),
     and unclassified-genus bins.
  4. Site Aggregation (`37` paired sites): Collapse multiple trips per site first,
     then report both **site means** (which preserve exact additivity
     `mean(Delta_Y) = mean(B) + mean(W) + mean(R)`) and **site medians** with
     2,000-draw site-bootstrap 95% CIs (`seed = 20261015`). For F1, run this
     decomposition on both `cpf_complete` (`775` MAGs) and `k01669_photolyase`
     (`975` MAGs) to show their concordance.

Test C — F4 Contig-Edge Accounting & Dual-Gate Soil Contrast:
  1. Partition all `822` complete `WEYH` `EctC` genes (and symmetrized SMAG/GEM
     comparators) into:
     (i)   `strict_complete_same_strand_ABC` (`EctA` and `EctB` both `partial=00`,
           same strand, within 5 kb, recording canonical gene orders such as
           `ectA-ectB-ectC` vs `ectA-ectB-ectC-ectD`),
     (ii)  `contig_edge_censored` (missing complete `EctA`/`EctB` and `<= 5 kb`
           from a contig end or flanking a contig-edge-truncated `EctA`/`EctB`),
     (iii) `internal_incomplete_or_orphan` (`> 5 kb` from both contig ends).
  2. Test the paired Surface-vs-Deep contrast (`37` sites / `69` visits) on
     `strict_complete_same_strand_ABC` carrier MAGs (primary new test, Holm-
     adjusted alongside the primary family-track ecology family) and check
     sensitivity on all complete `WEYH` `EctC` carrier MAGs so assembly breaks
     cannot masquerade as ecological niche differences.

4. REQUIRED INFERENCE CONTROLS VS. OPTIONAL SCOPE EXPANSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Required Inference Controls (Must Be Enforced):
  - F3: Explicit SMAG/GEM/reference taxonomy joins; reporting of unsupported/
    low-`n` phylum strata without extrapolation; pairwise source comparisons when
    3-way intersection has `n_p < 5`; trimmed 7TM-core vs full-length clustering
    comparison; symmetrical ORF completeness (`partial=00`), sequence length, and
    sequence-based 7TM topology/architecture checks across all sources.
  - F1 & F4: Exact 3-term additive decomposition (`B + W + R = Delta_Y`);
    hierarchical fallback for unknown genera; reporting both additive site-means
    and site-medians with site-bootstrap CIs; explicit statement that species-
    representative read mapping cannot resolve intra-species strain turnover.
  - F4: Symmetrical `complete EctC + WEYH + complete same-strand EctA/B` rules
    across EQ, SMAG, and GEM; contig-edge censoring check (`<= 5 kb`); gene-
    coordinate and domain-boundary audit of the 5 `EctCD` single-ORF candidates.
  - Interpretation Guardrails: Zero claims of enzymatic catalysis, retinal
    chromophore binding, phototaxis, or causal evolutionary selection from
    sequence, structure, or metagenomic abundance data.

B. Optional Scope Expansion (Not Required for Valid Inference; Defer or Bound):
  - F3 GPU 3D structure folding (AlphaFold/ESMFold on ORIX) and raw-read BAM
    remapping for heliorhodopsins: cannot be executed symmetrically on external
    SMAG/GEM protein pools, cannot establish photoreceptor/sensory activity, and
    is unnecessary to test whether taxonomic composition and terminal extensions
    explain the 50%-cluster count gap. Omit or restrict strictly to sequence-
    based TM/extension boundary annotation unless needed for a supplementary
    structural illustration.
  - F4 raw-read alignment check across the 5 `EctCD` inter-domain linkers:
    optional diagnostic only if pre-existing BAMs on IBEX allow a fast bounded
    Slurm check against assembly/frameshift artefacts; even if read-supported,
    report strictly as a continuous predicted `EctC-EctD` ORF, not a proven
    bifunctional enzyme.
  - F5 Carbonic Anhydrases: Keep stopped per the F5 stop rule; retain the
    audited catalogue in the supplement/archive only.

5. ASSESSMENT OF PLAUSIBLE MANUSCRIPT VALUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. F1 (CPF Light-Niche Decomposition) — HIGH SUPPORTING VALUE (Main Text
   Radiation/Repair Section + Supplement):
   Provides a rigorous complete-ORF profile census (`914` genes in `775` MAGs)
   and quantifies how much of the surface enrichment (`+8.47 pp`) reflects
   within-lineage enrichment (notably within `Actinomycetota`) vs community-wide
   phylum/genus turnover, while transparently documenting overlap with the
   `K01669` baseline and the short-photolyase (`QCI56913.1`) profile boundary.

2. F4 (Strict `ectABC` Neighborhood & Soil Partitioning) — HIGH SUPPORTING VALUE
   (Main Text Osmoprotection Section + Supplement):
   Strengthens the osmoprotection narrative by replacing single-marker `EctC`
   counts with a residue-verified (`WEYH`), complete-neighbor, same-strand
   `ectABC`/`ectABCD` architecture census (plus audited `EctCD` fusion leads)
   and testing whether canonical pathway carriers vary across surface vs deep
   horizons or track broad lineage composition.

3. F3 (Site-Conserving Heliorhodopsins) — MODEST / SUPPLEMENTARY VALUE ONLY
   (Methodological Caveat or Supplementary Resource):
   After retinal-site (`K241`) filtering reduced the native embedding gap by 69%
   (`0.01338` vs `0.00903`/`0.00918`) and left signaling-neighbor enrichment
   null (`5/18` vs `4/18`, `p = 1.0`; Type-1 `Holm p = 0.0781`), F3 is not a
   viable main-text sensory mechanism story. Test A has clear value as a concise
   supplementary control demonstrating how apparent environmental cluster
   "novelty" in desert MAGs is shaped by `Patescibacteriota` host representation,
   reference-panel bias, and N/C-terminal length extensions.

4. Overall Strategic Judgement:
   Executing the corrected F1, F4, and bounded F3 follow-ups on IBEX Slurm will
   make the manuscript's photoprotection, osmoprotection, and dark-matter
   sections substantially more defensible against referee scrutiny on taxonomic
   confounding and gene fragmentation, without overclaiming unmeasured
   biochemical functions.
