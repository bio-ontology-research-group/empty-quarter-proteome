3. TRACK F1 ASSESSMENT: CPF LIGHT-NICHE ECOLOGICAL DECOMPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Validity & What the Analyses Support
  - **Operational Definition & Non-Replication of Baseline Photolyase Result**:
    The F1 complete-profile census (`914` complete `partial=00` genes across
    `775` dereplicated EQ MAGs passing all three CPF Pfam domains) is a strict,
    non-exhaustive operational definition of full-length cryptochrome/photolyase
    family members (which excludes short photolyases lacking the standard three-
    domain profile architecture). Across the `550` valid CoverM profiles (`F5Dr1`
    properly excluded due to `100%` unmapped reads), `69` paired surface/deep
    visits at `37` sites yield a replicate/site-median surface-minus-deep
    enrichment of `+8.473` percentage points (`95%` site bootstrap CI
    `[+2.299, +13.554] pp`, raw Wilcoxon `p = 2.336e-4`, 6-contrast Holm
    `p = 0.001306`; `results/ecology/median_ecology_tests.tsv`). Because `747/775`
    (`96.4%`) of `CPF` carrier MAGs overlap the manuscript's existing `K01669`
    photolyase carrier set (`975` MAGs, `+8.799 pp`, raw `p = 2.176e-4`, Holm
    `p = 0.001306`) and `CPF_without_K01669` (`28` MAGs) shows zero soil-depth
    effect (`+0.0045 pp`, `CI [-0.015, +0.045] pp`, raw `p = 0.533`, Holm
    `p = 0.533`), F1 is **not an independent ecological replication**; it is a
    domain-completeness verification and taxonomic decomposition of the same
    metagenomic signal.
  - **Exact Three-Term Kitagawa Decomposition Resolves Phylum vs. Genus Turnover**:
    `scripts/ecology.py` implements the exact three-term decomposition on the
    replicate-mean relative-abundance simplex (`total_pp = between_pp + within_pp + exclusive_pp`
    verified to `< 1e-12` for all `69` visits and `< 1e-9` for all `37` site
    means in `audit.py`). Across `37` equally weighted sites, the arithmetic-mean
    surface-minus-deep CPF contrast is `+8.710 pp` (`95%` site bootstrap CI
    `[+4.192, +13.196] pp`; `decomposition_summary.tsv`):
      * **At Phylum rank**: Almost all community mass is in shared phyla
        (`common_mass = 99.05%`, `multi_MAG_mass = 99.99%`, `unclassified_mass = 0.0%`),
        and the enrichment appears predominantly **within phyla**
        (`within_pp = +9.298 pp` `[+5.801, +13.317]`, `between_pp = -0.865 pp`
        `[-2.813, +0.862]`, `exclusive_pp = +0.277 pp` `[-0.261, +1.104]`).
      * **At Genus rank**: Using the unique-MAG fallback for unclassified genera
        (`<phylum>;...;<family>;g__;unclassified_MAG=<mag>`), the apparent
        within-phylum enrichment resolves overwhelmingly into **between-lineage
        compositional turnover across genera**: `between_pp = +5.305 pp`
        (`[+1.209, +9.016]`, `60.9%` of total) plus `exclusive_pp = +2.378 pp`
        (`[+0.905, +3.889]`, `27.3%` of total) together account for `+7.683 pp`
        (`88.2%` of the `+8.710 pp` total), whereas `within_pp` (shifts among
        species within shared multi-MAG genera) contributes only `+1.027 pp`
        (`[+0.685, +1.399]`, `11.8%` of total). `K01669` shows the exact same
        pattern (`total = +10.103 pp`: genus `between = +6.241 pp`,
        `exclusive = +2.851 pp`, `within = +1.010 pp`).

3.2 Taxonomic Resolution Limits & Fixed-Trait Guardrails
  - **Fixed Binary Species-Representative Traits Preclude Within-Strain Claims**:
    CoverM maps reads to `5,438` static ANI95 species-representative MAGs with
    fixed binary trait calls (`0` or `1`). Consequently, the genus-level
    `within_pp` term (`+1.027 pp`) measures **differential relative abundance of
    carrier vs. non-carrier species within multi-species genera**, NOT within-
    species or within-strain evolutionary adaptation, horizontal gene transfer,
    or transcriptional regulation across soil depths.
  - **Structural Effect of Unclassified Genera (`25.70%` Community Mass)**:
    Assigning each `g__` MAG to its own singleton group (`unclassified_MAG=<mag>`)
    rightly prevents the ecological fallacy of pooling unrelated taxa into a
    single `"Unknown"` genus. However, because every singleton group has only
    `1` MAG, its carrier fraction `p_g` is identically `0` or `1` in every sample,
    making `p_{g,S} - p_{g,D} = 0` (`W_g = 0` by construction). Since
    unclassified-genus MAGs comprise `25.70%` of mean community abundance
    (`95% CI [23.68%, 27.71%]`; `multi_MAG_mass = 67.47%`; `common_mass = 84.87%`),
    any hypothetical intrageneric species turnover inside those unannotated
    genera is mathematically absorbed into `between_pp` (when the MAG is present
    in both horizons) or `exclusive_pp` (when present in only one horizon). Root
    notes this limitation accurately in `ROOT_RESULTS_JUDGEMENT.txt`, and it
    must be stated explicitly in the Methods and supplement.

4.2 Critical Finding on `complete_A == complete_B` Loci & Ecological Null Result
  - **7 EQ Loci and 1 GEM Locus Satisfy `complete_A` and `complete_B` via a
    Single Neighboring ORF (`ectAB` Fusion Candidates)**:
    Inspection of `results/f4_integrity/loci.tsv` reveals that in `f4_integrity.py`
    (lines 41–51), `chosen['A']` and `chosen['B']` are selected independently
    without requiring `chosen['A'] != chosen['B']`. In `7/616` EQ `strict_ABC`
    loci (all `7` also passing `strict_ABC_cov70`, meaning both `TIGR02406` and
    `TIGR02407` hit the same ORF at `>= 70%` profile coverage) and `1/47` GEM
    `strict_ABC` loci (`3300025185_2_826`), `complete_A` and `complete_B` are
    the **exact same protein ID** (e.g., `S55PRr1_155462_10`, `55Sr1_106407_6`,
    `3PRr3_2920480_20`). Moreover, when such a locus lies on the negative
    strand (`strand == -1`), sorting `[(start, 'A'), (start, 'B')]` with
    `reverse=True` flips the tie-breaking order to `'B-A-C-D'`, accounting for
    all `4` `'B-A-C-D'` entries in `EQ_MAG`! Consequently, among the `616` EQ
    `strict_ABC` loci, `609` (`605` at `cov70`) comprise distinct `ectA`, `ectB`,
    and `ectC` ORFs (`405 A-B-C-D`, `205 A-B-C`, `1 B-C-D-A`, `1 B-C-A-D` minus
    the `3` plus-strand `ectAB` fusions), whereas `7` comprise a single complete
    `ectAB` two-domain ORF adjacent to `ectC` (`+/- ectD`). This does not
    invalidate their inclusion as complete `ectABC` neighborhoods, but the
    distinction between `609` three-gene `ectA+ectB+ectC` neighborhoods and `7`
    fused-neighbor `ectAB+ectC` neighborhoods must be stated explicitly if gene-
    order or strict-locus counts are reported.
  - **Primary Median Ecological Test is Nonsignificant; Descriptive Mean
    Bootstrap Must Not Override It**:
    Under the prespecified primary paired soil-horizon test (`replicate medians`
    -> `site median surface-minus-deep differences` across `37` sites / `69`
    visits -> two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test with 6-contrast Holm
    correction; `results/ecology/median_ecology_tests.tsv`), `EctABC_strict`
    (`608` MAGs) yields a median difference of `+0.559` percentage points
    (`95%` site bootstrap CI `[+0.113, +2.554] pp`), with **raw Wilcoxon
    `p = 0.0974` and Holm-adjusted `p = 0.3895`** (identically, `EctABC_cov70`
    [`604` MAGs] has median `+0.559 pp`, raw `p = 0.0974`, Holm `p = 0.3895`;
    `EctC_WEYH` [`779` MAGs] has median `+0.256 pp`, raw `p = 0.1579`, Holm
    `p = 0.3895`).
    In `results/ecology/decomposition_summary.tsv` and `results/figures/fig_family_ecology.pdf`,
    the separate **arithmetic-mean** decomposition estimand yields `mean = +2.099 pp`
    with a 95% site bootstrap CI of `[+0.225, +4.163] pp` (at genus rank:
    `exclusive_pp = +1.004 pp` `[+0.225, +2.056]`, `between_pp = +0.892 pp`
    `[-0.430, +2.333]`, `within_pp = +0.203 pp` `[-0.268, +0.817]`), because a
    subset of right-skewed sites pulls the site mean above the site median
    (`+2.099 pp` mean vs `+0.889 pp` median of replicate-mean differences and
    `+0.559 pp` median of replicate-median differences). As `ROOT_RESULTS_JUDGEMENT.txt`
    properly recognizes, **this descriptive mean bootstrap interval cannot
    override the nonsignificant primary Wilcoxon test (`p = 0.0974`, `Holm p = 0.3895`)**.
    Crucially, because the right panel of `fig_family_ecology.pdf` (`EctABC strict`)
    plots *only* the mean decomposition—where the `total` error bar (`[+0.225, +4.163]`)
    visually excludes zero—presenting that figure without annotating the
    nonsignificant primary median test (`+0.559 pp`, `p = 0.0974`, `Holm p = 0.3895`)
    directly on the panel or in its title/caption would mislead readers into
    inferring significant surface enrichment.
