New evidence after R1:
- IBEX job 53678755 extracted covariates and SHA-256 hashes for 212,084 indexed family FASTAs. `selection_covariates.py` is the extraction code. `match_selection.py` performs fixed-seed coarsened exact matching using dS, CDS length, GC and usable sequence count; the dN/dS outcome is not used for matching. It retains 61,427 per arm and reverses the pooled median difference (+0.0369 to -0.0367). Residual dS SMD is -0.124. No phylogenetic matching or alignment-error correction is claimed.
- IBEX job 53678768 extracts MAG coding intervals and runs minimap2 against each paired culture-derived genome. `culture_nucleotide_check.py`, `culture_nucleotide/summary.json`, `per_gene.tsv` and `input_sha256.json` document the run. 347/1,105 protein-unmatched eligible dark calls have >=90% query coverage at >=95% nucleotide identity. Recovery in protein-shared calls is only ~73%; negatives remain inconclusive. This tests DNA presence, not translation. A harmonised gene-caller comparison is still open.
- `round2_checks.py` recomputes structural threshold sensitivity and zero-score decomposition from pinned inputs, and frame-arm attrition (9,029 both, 119 null-only, 153 annotated-only, 699 neither). Zero scores are not assumed equivalent to exhaustive structural absence. Raw structural result rows still need explicit membership verification.
- `make_review_figures.py` replaces Figures 1--4 with current compact-table plots. Original source figures remain preserved; main citations/captions were updated. Figures 5--6 and environmental models remain from the earlier analysis and deserve scrutiny.
- `papers/round2/manifest.tsv` contains additional Flash-guided targeted literature. Root independently checked all 27 XML embedded DOI identities available at this point; none mismatched. Read texts, not just summaries. The DOI resolver is updating the canonical verified manifest.
