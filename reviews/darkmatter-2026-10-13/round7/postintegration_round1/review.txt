The manuscript titled "Known phyla, unknown proteome: a controlled census of the Rub' al-Khali soil metagenome" presents a metagenomic and structural census of 573 hyperarid soil and root-zone samples from the Empty Quarter, linking 14,426 refined bins (5,438 ANI95 species representatives across 51 named phyla in GTDB R232) to a sequence- and structure-dark proteome analysis. In this post-integration revision, the authors have integrated four targeted extension analyses (H1 through H4) across the Abstract, Results, Discussion, Methods, Supplementary Discussion S3, and a new Figure 4: a phylogeny-aware PGLS analysis of sequence-dark gene fraction across 237 Thermoplasmatota representatives (H1), a 1,461-family genomic neighbourhood conservation analysis with a 353-family complete-window primary cohort (H2), a length-distribution-matched and caller-harmonized DIAMOND benchmark of 106,042 dark and 106,042 control queries against SMAG and GEM reference databases (H3), and a paired whole-chain versus high-confidence cropped-region Foldseek PDB experiment across 5,851 ESMFold models (H4).

My overall verdict on the newly integrated H1-H4 sections and Figure 4 is ACCEPT_WITH_BOUNDS. Independent verification of the H1-H4 source tables, raw IBEX audit closures (including H3 raw audit job 53796328 across all 212,084 queries, 7 matched FASTA pairs, 14 DIAMOND databases, and 16 hit files, and H4 GPU raw audit job 53796103 across 6,081,552 alignment rows), Foldseek C++ source code, and manuscript claims confirms that all previously withdrawn alignment-normalised TM-score decomposition claims and heuristic rescaling ratios have been thoroughly removed, that all numerical denominators and test statistics in the manuscript match the production outputs, and that all mechanistic interpretations (phylogenetic covariance in H1, conditional complete-window synteny in H2, catalogue matching without causal attribution in H3, and joint length/content/retrieval effects in H4) are properly bounded for Nature Communications. Below I list six prioritized, straightforward text, methods-detail, and reproducibility-script refinements to tighten internal clarity before round-2 closure, followed by minor notes and a separate summary of remaining paper-wide submission prerequisites.

Comments:

1. H4 and Figure 4b/c numerical completeness for the "Other dark" cohort and transition categories (Results lines 332-355, Figure 4 caption lines 391-403, Supplementary Discussion S3 lines 1130-1138).
In Results (lines 335-349), the manuscript states: "We therefore pooled all 1,031 confidence- and length-qualified dark models with initial query-normalised TM-score < 0.5 into a single low-query-score dark group (984 with at least one eligible high-confidence region) and compared whole-chain and cropped-region searches executed in the same software environment against the 30 July 2026 PDB snapshot (5,851 models and 7,773 cropped regions in total, including 1,807 other confidence-qualified dark models and 3,013 known-fold controls; Fig. 4a,b)... In the primary CPU Tier 1 analysis of the 1,031 initially low-query-score dark models, 67 matched PDB as whole chains and 964 did not; cropping recovered a PDB match at query-normalised TM-score >= 0.5 for 714 of the 964 whole-chain-unmatched models (74.07%), bringing the total with at least one cropped-region match to 775 of 1,031 (75.17%; Fig. 4b,c)."
In Figure 4b (manuscript/figures/fig_structure_current.pdf), the bar chart plots whole-chain and cropped-region match rates for all three groups: "Initially low query score" (n = 1,031: 6.5% whole-chain, 75.2% cropped), "Other dark" (n = 1,807: 77.9% whole-chain, 87.3% cropped), and "Controls" (n = 3,013: 95.5% whole-chain, 95.9% cropped), while Figure 4c plots the breakdown of the 964 whole-chain-unmatched models (714 recovered, 167 low score, 38 zero hits, 0 excluded, 45 no region). Three small additions will make the text and Figure 4 self-contained:
First, neither Results nor S3 states the exact numerator/denominator counts plotted for the "Other dark" bars (1,408/1,807 = 77.92% whole-chain matched and 1,578/1,807 = 87.33% cropped-region matched in reviews/darkmatter-2026-10-13/round7/root_checks/h4_outputs_preclosure/v7_stratum_census.tsv, tier_1 row Q_not_K_all) or for the Control whole-chain bar (2,878/3,013 = 95.52%, tier_1 row Controls).
Second, a reader comparing Figure 4a and Figure 4b will ask why "Other dark" (defined in Figure 4a as the 1,807 models that already achieved query-normalised TM-score >= 0.5 in the initial dual-reference screen) has 77.9% rather than 100% whole-chain matches in Figure 4b, and why 67/1,031 (6.50%) of the "Initially low query score" group match as whole chains in Figure 4b. As verified in v7_stratum_census.tsv and v7_model_summaries.tsv, this occurs because the initial screen searched both PDB and AFDB v4 at --max-seqs 50, whereas the paired experiment searches only the 30 July 2026 PDB snapshot at deeper ranking (--max-seqs 1000, adaptive 5000): within Q_not_K_all (1,807), the 1,107 models with an initial PDB match (Q_not_K_pdb) match 100% (1,107/1,107) as whole chains and 91.15% (1,009/1,107) after cropping, whereas the 700 models whose initial match came only from AFDB (Q_not_K_afdb_only) have 301/700 (43.00%) whole-chain PDB matches and rise to 569/700 (81.29%) after cropping (recovering 292 of 399 whole-chain-unmatched models). Conversely, 67 of the 1,031 initial low-score models reach query-normalised TM-score >= 0.5 against PDB as whole chains when --max-seqs is increased from 50 to 1,000. Explaining this in one sentence in Results or the Figure 4 caption (and reporting the Q_not_K_pdb and Q_not_K_afdb_only breakdown in S3) resolves an obvious question for attentive readers.
Third, in S3 (lines 1134-1138), the text defines the five whole-chain-unmatched and three whole-chain-matched transition categories verbally without giving their counts. Reporting the exact Tier 1 counts for the 1,031 low-query-score dark models in S3 (among 964 whole-chain-unmatched: 714 recovered, 167 persistent low score, 38 raw zero retrieval, 0 post-exclusion empty, 45 no eligible region; among 67 whole-chain-matched: 61 retained in crop, 4 lost to low score, 2 lacking an eligible region) directly links S3 to Figure 4c.

2. H4 Supplementary Discussion S3 completeness on row-removal sensitivity for out-of-range alignment scores (S3 lines 1166-1168).
In Supplementary Discussion S3 (lines 1166-1168), the manuscript states: "Removing CPU rows with out-of-range alignment scores left the primary whole-chain and crop match classifications unchanged; stricter exclusion tiers changed crop classifications for nine distinct control models."
In reviews/darkmatter-2026-10-13/round7/root_checks/h4_exclusion_fact_audit.json, removing entire rows with alntmscore > 1 leaves Tier 0 and Tier 1 classifications unchanged (0 whole-chain and 0 crop changes), but under the stricter sequence-identity exclusion tiers (Tiers 2-4) it changes whole-chain classifications for four distinct control models (wc_status_changes.unique_queries = 4: 3 models each in Tiers 2 and 4, and 4 models in Tier 3) in addition to changing crop classifications for nine distinct control models (model_crop_status_changes.unique_models = 9: 8 models each in Tiers 2 and 4, and 9 models in Tier 3). Updating lines 1166-1168 to state that stricter exclusion tiers changed whole-chain classifications for four distinct control models and crop classifications for nine distinct control models makes this sensitivity statement exact.

3. H2 Methods and Supplementary Discussion S3 self-containment for locus tie-breaking, permutation seeds, and Mantel-Haenszel stratification (Methods lines 734-744, S3 lines 1084-1090).
In Methods (lines 734-736), the manuscript states: "Within each family and dRep species cluster, one primary locus was selected using the archived deterministic ordering that favours complete windows and longer contigs without inspecting flank KO identities." And in S3 (lines 1084-1088), it states: "Stratifying by species breadth, taxonomic group and flank annotation opportunity, the Mantel-Haenszel excess recurrence contrast was Delta_MH(E_fam) = 0.035358 (10,000 permutations, p = 0.4069)."
To make the H2 protocol fully reproducible from the manuscript text without consulting the scripts (02_extract_neighbourhoods.py lines 837-846 and 03_analyse_conservation.py in reviews/darkmatter-2026-10-13/round7/snapshots/h2_final/scripts/), please spell out: (a) in Methods lines 734-736, the exact eight-key deterministic tie-break order used within each family and species cluster (primary-window eligibility, stop-codon-rescue window eligibility, exact-mapped flank count, exact-or-stop3 mapped flank count, present-flank count, contig length in bp, contig gene count descending, then member identifier ascending) and the three random seeds (20261013 for the 5,000 same-contig positional null draws, 20261014 for the 10,000 primary label permutations, and 20261015 for the 10,000 Tier 2 stop-rescue permutations); and (b) in S3 lines 1084-1088, the exact 16-stratum cross-classification (species breadth 2 versus >= 3, taxonomic group Thermoplasmatota, Actinomycetota, multi-phylum, or other bacteria/archaea, and flank KO annotation opportunity above versus at/below the pooled median of 0.375 in Tier 1 and 0.350 in Tier 2, weighted by n_K * n_QnotK / (n_K + n_QnotK)).

4. H3 Supplementary Discussion S3 sequence-overlap accounting between pre-matching and post-matching pools (S3 lines 1113-1116).
In Supplementary Discussion S3 (lines 1113-1116), the manuscript states: "The primary pair retained 22,310 exact sequences in both arms. Removing all shared sequences before matching left 4,185,525 proteins and 1,289,129,312 residues per arm and yielded a +18.62 percentage-point dark excess."
Compared against Results line 409 ("4,210,592 proteins and 1,295,970,055 residues per arm"), a reader subtracting 4,210,592 - 4,185,525 = 25,067 will notice that the reduction in per-arm matched proteins is 25,067 rather than 22,310. As recorded in reviews/darkmatter-2026-10-13/round7/snapshots/h3_completed/matching_closure_audit.tsv (rows harmonized_seed20261014 and harmonized_seed20261014_no_overlap), 25,067 exact sequences were shared between the full deduplicated SMAG and GEM candidate pools prior to length-quota sampling (pre_matching_shared_seqs = 25067), of which 22,310 were sampled into both arms in the primary draw (post_matching_shared_seqs = 22310). Stating that removing all 25,067 pre-matching shared sequences (of which 22,310 had been retained in both arms of the primary draw) left 4,185,525 proteins and 1,289,129,312 residues per arm makes the arithmetic transparent.
