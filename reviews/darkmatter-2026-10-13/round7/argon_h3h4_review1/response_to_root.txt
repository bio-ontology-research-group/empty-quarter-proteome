1. Unreserved Withdrawal of Initial Comments 5-6 alnTM Rescaling and Heuristic Boundary Claims
Upon inspecting reviews/darkmatter-2026-10-13/round7/h4_tm_anomaly/source/TMaligner.cpp (lines 50-210) and the hit-aggregation logic in reviews/darkmatter-2026-10-13/round7/h4_postprocess_repairs/v7_recovery_postprocess.py (lines 283-388) and v7_crop_and_search_regions.py (lines 559-585), Root's technical critique of Comments 5-6 in our initial review.txt is 100% correct. We withdraw our prior alnTM rescaling and boundary claims on three independent grounds:
   (a) Non-linear dependence of TM-score optimization on normalizationLen in TMaligner.cpp:
       In TMaligner::computeAppoximateTMscore (TMaligner.cpp lines 89-103), normalizationLen is passed directly into parameter_set4search(normalizationLen, normalizationLen, D0_MIN, Lnorm, score_d8, d0, d0_search, dcu0), which determines the distance scale d0, the inclusion radius score_d8, and the search radius d0_search used inside standard_TMscore (line 95) and detailed_search_standard (line 100) to optimize the Kabsch superposition (rotation matrix u and translation vector t). In TMaligner::computeExactTMscore (lines 145-201), normalizationLen similarly governs parameter_set4search (line 145), the residue-pair filter d <= score_d8 (line 165), KabschFast (line 188), parameter_set4final (line 194), and TMscore8_search (line 198). Because changing normalizationLen from L - 1 to L alters d0, score_d8, and the optimal superposition trajectory itself, multiplying reported alntm by (L - 1) / L is NOT an exact recalculation of computeTMscore(..., L), and L / (L - 1) is NOT a validated mathematical upper bound on score shifts or threshold crossings.
   (b) Cross-hit mixing in v7_model_summaries.tsv and top-qTM conditioning in v7_region_evaluations.tsv:
       In v7_recovery_postprocess.py (lines 377-386), wc_t{k}_alntm is taken from wa["top_alntm"][k] (the hit independently maximizing alnTM), whereas wc_t{k}_qspan_cov and wc_t{k}_fident are taken from wa["top_qtm"][k] (the hit maximizing qTM). Those two fields can belong to different target structures and alignments. In v7_region_evaluations.tsv (lines 305-325), t1_alntm and t1_qspan both come from ca_info["top_qtm"][1] (the top-qTM hit rather than the top-alnTM hit) and record only qspan rather than min(qspan, tspan). Consequently, our prior count of "1 WC model and 14 cropped regions crossing 0.5" combined incompatible fields or non-maximizing hits and is withdrawn in full.
   (c) Unknown historical alignment spans in per_rep_structures.tsv:
       The historical baseline table per_rep_structures.tsv does not store alignment spans min(qspan, tspan), and no minimum 50 aa or 100 aa alignment-span bound applies to all baseline whole-chain alignments. Therefore, the heuristic bands [0.5, 0.5051] and [0.5, 0.5102] are not validated upper bounds on membership changes among K_local_pdb (849), K_local_afdb_only (87), and K_dual_none (95). No scientific claim in the manuscript or Supplement may rest on post-hoc rescaled alnTM values or heuristic alnTM boundary counts.

3. Required Scientific and Methodological Bounds for the Pooled-qTM Manuscript Revision
We fully endorse Root's proposed manuscript restructuring under the following five strict bounds:
   (a) Remove active historical alnTM-threshold counts and subdivisions from scientific claims:
       Remove the historical alnTM >= 0.5 vs < 0.5 subdivisions and counts (95 dual-none, 936 local-match, 849 K_local_pdb, 87 K_local_afdb_only, 797 high-coverage local matches, 68 controls < 0.5) from active biological/structural claims in the main text. Relegate the historical 3-way subdivision of K_pooled to archived pipeline/software diagnostics only, and retain all original raw Foldseek scores unmodified on disk.
   (b) Compare whole-chain and cropped-region searches strictly within the same Round 7 Foldseek run against the frozen PDB target:
       Because the Round 7 high-sensitivity search (-s 9.5 --max-seqs 1000) already matches 67/1,031 (6.50%) of K_pooled and 301/700 (43.00%) of Q_not_K_afdb_only at the whole-chain level against the frozen PDB database, all whole-chain vs cropped-region comparisons must contrast the Round 7 whole-chain PDB search (67/1,031 matched, 964/1,031 unmatched in K_pooled; 1,475/2,838 matched, 1,363/2,838 unmatched in total Q) against the Round 7 cropped PDB search (775/1,031 matched, 714/964 WC-unmatched recovered in K_pooled; 2,353/2,838 matched, 1,006/1,363 WC-unmatched recovered in total Q).
   (c) State the exact scope of qTM's immunity to the structureconvertalis.cpp:898 defect:
       Document in Methods/Supplement that in Foldseek 10-941cd33, OUTFMT_ALNTMSCORE (structureconvertalis.cpp:898) passes normalizationLen = min(res.qEndPos - res.qStartPos, res.dbEndPos - res.dbStartPos) = min(qspan - 1, tspan - 1) (missing + 1 on 0-indexed inclusive coordinates), which perturbs parameter_set4search, d0, score_d8, and superposition optimization inside TMaligner.cpp (producing 2,194 raw rows with alntm > 1.00001 across 228 control queries in Round 7, plus 47 PDB and 85 AFDB control rows > 1.0 in historical per_control_structures.tsv.gz). State that OUTFMT_QTMSCORE (line 903, passing res.qLen) is unaffected by this specific alignment-span off-by-one defect, without claiming that Foldseek qTM is exact TM-align or free of all approximations.
   (d) Do not claim that confident-region cropping isolates a single causal mechanism:
       State explicitly that cropping contiguous smoothed-pLDDT >= 0.70 regions (>= 50 aa) jointly changes (i) query length normalization L_q and the length-dependent TM-score distance scale d_0(L_q), (ii) query structural content (removing low-pLDDT segments and isolating individual regions), and (iii) search and multiple-testing opportunity (evaluating multiple cropped regions per chain and altering prefilter candidate ranking). The H4 result establishes the empirical magnitude of domain-level qTM >= 0.5 recovery under confident-region cropping (714/964 = 74.07% unconditional; 714/919 = 77.69% eligible in K_pooled), not an isolated single-factor mechanism.
   (e) Pending raw IBEX/ORIX audits by Root:
       Root's raw audit jobs 53796102 (H3 raw audit) and 53796103 (H4 ORIX GPU raw audit) are currently executing on IBEX and remain required for final raw-level closure before manuscript lock.
