# IBEX review mirror scope

Destination: `/ibex/scratch/projects/c2014/rob/metagenomics/06_darkmatter/p7_analyses_clean/manuscript_review_20261014/`.

This preserves the revised manuscript, figures, round7 reviews and raw-audit receipts/code, H1 final snapshot, H1–H4 compact inputs, round7 execution scripts, top-level reproducibility code/ledgers and the retained literature collection. The SHA-256 inventory checks exact transferred bytes on a Slurm worker. It is an evidence mirror, not a claim that all upstream analyses were rerun inside this directory.

Heavy H3 reference genomes, FASTAs, indexes and hits, H4 CPU and ORIX GPU hits, and H1/H2 upstream inputs remain at their production paths in `reproducibility/darkmatter/round7_analyses/README.md`. The mirror avoids duplicating those datasets. The full current-paper driver also depends on earlier compact/source snapshots outside this selected mirror; retain the local repository package and original IBEX provenance paths.

Whole-paper raw-read access, duplicate-run merging provenance, public ENA access, MAG deposition and release DOI remain open. These limits are stated in the manuscript and are separate from successful extension-specific checks.
