# Completed-results review contract

The reviewer must evaluate actual executed code and data, not just proposed hypotheses or a runnable workflow. Nature Communications is the editorial target, not an assumed acceptance outcome.

For each H1-H4 distinguish execution completeness, numerical correctness, statistical validity, scientific support, and main-text versus supplementary fit. A scientifically informative null result is acceptable. Unresolved public availability must remain explicit.

H1: all 237 Thermoplasmatota genomes, phylogenetic covariance and conditional interpretation; completed round6 evidence may be reused with immutable hashes.
H2: all 1461 eligible families, sequence-verified context, attrition, positional background, covariate overlap and dependence limitations; avoid treating fixed-outcome species reweighting as full cluster bootstrap.
H3: broad seeded sampling across the full public SMAG frame, genuine effort to harmonize callers on source DNA, exact canonical protein size/length matching, all 212084 queries, provenance and repeated comparator-draw sensitivities. A caller-confounded release comparison is not a causal biome result.
H4: all 5851 models, paired whole/cropped alignments, confident-region eligibility, symmetric source/identity exclusions and control calibration, retrieval saturation audit, actual aligned residue-pair coverage and exact census denominators. Nonretrieval cannot establish novelty.

Review outputs: plain-text report; machine-readable verdicts for each hypothesis with critical/major/minor issues, specific evidence paths, necessary fixes, supported and unsupported claims, recommended paper placement. Do not certify missing work. Root audits and frozen input/output manifests form part of the evidence. Re-review repaired findings with the prior review and explicit change record.
