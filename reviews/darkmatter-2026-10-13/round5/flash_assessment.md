# Root assessment of Flash scouting

Flash ran through agy with gemini-3.8-flash-high; the SUCCESS receipt and unedited
report are retained. Its small exploratory scripts are proposals, not authoritative
scientific evidence. Root selected and recorded three post hoc designs before
running them on IBEX: caller substitution, sampling-conditioned expedition detection,
and paired functional-profile nulls/threshold sensitivity (see DESIGN.md).

Rejected interpretations:
- A ratio of separately maximised qTM and alignment-TM scores is not alignment
  coverage. The maxima may refer to different targets and normalisations differ.
  Neither this ratio nor score discordance proves multidomain architecture or
  absence of novel folds. Existing matched-hit coverage diagnostics remain relevant.
- Flash's median-prevalence urn calculation cannot serve as an aggregate null for
  106,042 heterogeneous families. Root instead permuted the actual full matrix.
- DNA alignments pass >=95% identity; they are not necessarily identical DNA.
  Caller substitution tests pipeline sensitivity, not genuine translation or a
  particular Bakta filtering mechanism. Complete >=100-aa calls use the original
  protein-length field, not the approximate >=300-nt filter in Flash's script.
  Gene-level Fisher tests ignore genome-pair dependence and are not promoted.
- Quality-stratified Thermoplasmatota summaries alone do not exclude fragmentation,
  lineage or reference-distance explanations. Placeholder names do not validate
  taxonomic novelty. No such mechanistic claim is added.
- Predictor-only KOs are not established false positives. Abundance thresholds are
  a fixed exploratory sensitivity grid, not optimisation against MAG ground truth.

All three selected tests are reported together, including negative results. The
Argon review receives root-verified outputs, source code and this adjudication.
