# Four-extension development and Argon viability review

Author request: do all four extensions using agy, send them to Argon, and refine
until Argon agrees that each is a viable hypothesis. Nature Communications remains
the target. The purpose of this stage is to execute feasibility analyses and produce
four defensible, testable studies; a viable hypothesis need not have a positive result.

The four independent agy Flash High work sessions own separate areas under
reproducibility/darkmatter/round6_hypotheses/. They can run CPU pilots through IBEX
Slurm and must preserve original inputs, code, hashes, commands and job receipts.
The manuscript and earlier analyses remain unchanged while designs are investigated.
Root checks the evidence and sends a frozen dossier/code/result bundle to agy
Gemini 4 Argon High. Every review and response is retained. Critical objections
trigger corrected analyses or designs and another review of the actual revision.

Acceptance requires an explicit Argon judgment on each hypothesis, tied to an
identified dossier revision, and root verification of the supporting evidence:

1. A falsifiable question and an identifiable primary estimand.
2. Actual accessible inputs, unambiguous population/identifier joins and a measured
   feasibility check of the critical data dependency.
3. A runnable analysis with specified controls, dependence, missingness, thresholds,
   selection rules and interpretable positive and negative outcomes.
4. A clear distinction between executed pilot, completed analysis and proposed work.
5. No unresolved critical design objection disguised as a positive result or an
   untested assumption. Scientific limitations can remain if they bound an honest,
   narrower estimand; any reduction from the original scope must be explicit.
6. Consistent scope with the neighbouring phage, amplicon and life-on-air manuscripts.

Argon is free to reject any design. Root will not ask it to rubber-stamp proposals
or remove limitations to elicit approval. Incorrect reviewer claims are corrected
with primary artifacts in the response and returned for review. Agreement is a
methodological milestone, not evidence that a hypothesis is true or publishable.

Baseline manuscript and integrity scan are retained here. The only PDF scan flags
are the legitimate white heatmap labels 69, 90 and 61; there is no reviewer-directed
instruction. No public release, manuscript submission or communication to people
is part of this task.
