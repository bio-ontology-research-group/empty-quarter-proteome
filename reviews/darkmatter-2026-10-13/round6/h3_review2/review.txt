## 5. Non-Critical Documentation Notes and Optional Improvements

1. **Ancillary E-Value Rescaling Diagnostic Is a Rough Heuristic, Not an Exact Decomposition (`DOSSIER.md:25,60-65`, `DESIGN.md:25`, `scripts/09_v3_match_gem_and_search.py:601-650`, `results/v3_top1_vs_dbsize_decomposition.tsv`):**
   - To explain the discrepancy in the preliminary 2,500-genome GEM benchmark (`03_pilot_subsample_search.py`, Job `53700569`), Section 3 divides the E-value threshold by the raw protein-count ratio $122,978,191 / 5,815,177 = 21.15\times$ ($E_{\text{sub}} \le 4.73 \times 10^{-5}$) and labels the `79/110` (`71.82%`) dark and `885/895` (`98.88%`) control hits below that cutoff as "genuine `-k 1` secondary-hit masking" versus `31/110` (`28.18%`) and `10/895` (`1.12%`) as "database-size E-value rescaling."
   - In Karlin-Altschul / DIAMOND statistics ($E = K m' n' e^{-\lambda S}$), E-values scale with the **effective searched amino-acid residue space** $n' = N_{\text{letters}} - N_{\text{seqs}} \cdot l_{\text{eff}}(m)$ (which depends on total database letters and query-length edge correction $l_{\text{eff}}(m)$), composition-based adjustments/masking, and DIAMOND's seed/extension/block heuristics—not a uniform sequence-count ratio (note also `09_v3_match_gem_and_search.py:605` comments `1,835,451,488` sub2500 letters whereas `logs/pilot_53700569.err:23` reports `1,834,739,150`).
   - **Distinction from Primary Design:** This calculation is purely ancillary to motivating why historical `-k 1` tables cannot be subsampled post-hoc. In the primary v3 experiment (`09_v3_match_gem_and_search.py:266-405`), both SMAG and GEM matched databases are freshly built and searched with **identical unique protein counts, identical total amino-acid residues, and identical exact integer-length histograms**, so effective search space $n'$ is identically matched for every query. When citing Section 3 in manuscript or supplement text, describe the $4.73 \times 10^{-5}$ split as an approximate heuristic diagnostic (or pass `--dbsize` directly in a benchmark if an exact decomposition is desired).
