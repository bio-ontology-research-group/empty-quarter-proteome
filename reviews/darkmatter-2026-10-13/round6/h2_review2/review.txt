* **Scope Changes from Original (`round5/EXTENSIONS.md` H2):**
  1. Separate within-arm recurrence relative to a same-contig eligible-position reference from the between-arm covariate-adjusted contrast on background-adjusted excess $E_{\text{fam}}$, and restrict interpretation strictly to computational flanking KO colocalisation (excluding novel-fold, operon co-transcription, gene expression, mobile-element exclusion, and adaptive claims).

## 5. Non-Blocking Methodological Caveats & Optional Improvements (Targeted Dossier Audit)

While the bounded positional-reference and stratified excess-recurrence design is viable and executable, four overstatements in `DOSSIER.md` should be tempered when reporting the pilot or executing the full 1,461-family Gate 3 run:

1. **O1 (Species-Block Bootstrap Implementation & Conditional-Exchangeability Phrasing; `scripts/03_analyse_conservation.py:859-883`, `DOSSIER.md:152-154`):**
   * The physical locus audit (`170 / 170` Tier 1 eligible primary loci on `170` distinct `(bin, contig)` pairs with `0` shared contigs or overlapping 4-flank windows) proves physical window disjointness. However, the species-cluster bootstrap in `scripts/03_analyse_conservation.py:863-882` resamples the `144` unique dRep species clusters with replacement only by reweighting each family's *fixed* pre-computed $E_{\text{fam}}$ and $C_{\text{fam}}$ by its mean species multiplicity $w_F = \frac{1}{|S_F|}\sum_{s \in S_F} m_s^*$ and computing the *unstratified* arm difference (`+0.0989` baseline) rather than recomputing multi-species KO recurrence ($\geq 2$ distinct resampled species) or the primary Mantel-Haenszel stratified estimator ($\Delta_{\text{MH}}(E_{\text{fam}}) = -0.0648$). Similarly, the 40-family zero-shared-species sensitivity (`26 K, 14 QnotK`, unadjusted $\Delta E_{\text{fam}} = +0.1091$, Mann-Whitney $p = 0.5452$, Welch $t$ $p = 0.4036$) is an unadjusted sub-cohort check, while across the 72 Tier 1 families `21 / 144` dRep species (`8` across both arms), `13 / 57` named GTDB genera, and `19 / 56` named GTDB families are shared by $\geq 2$ structural families.
   * **Recommendation:** Frame conditional exchangeability and cross-family independence as *identifying assumptions and sensitivity checks* rather than properties that were "verified" (`DOSSIER.md:152`) or proven not to "drive or mask" conclusions (`DOSSIER.md:154`), and note that 4-bin R232 phylum grouping (`Thermoplasmatota`, `Actinomycetota`, `multi`, `other_bacteria_archaea`) and 9-pair exact phylum matching provide coarse taxonomic conditioning rather than full phylogenetic covariance control.
2. **O2 (Unsupported Power Claim in Full-Cohort Recipe; `DOSSIER.md:225`):**
   * `DOSSIER.md:225` asserts "$\geq 80\%$ power for $|\Delta_{\text{MH}}(E_{\text{fam}})| \geq 0.12\text{--}0.15$ at $\alpha = 0.05$" for the full 1,461-family Gate 3 run (`sbatch scripts/run_pilot.sbatch full 1461 results_full false`), but no power calculation script or output is saved in `scripts/` or `results/`. Under an infinite-superpopulation normal approximation scaling the observed pilot null SDs (`0.0937` for $\Delta_{\text{MH}}(E_{\text{fam}})$, `0.0965` for $\Delta_{\text{MH}}(C_{\text{fam}})$, `0.0961` for OLS/HC3) by the expected Gate 3 Tier 1 arm sizes ($570 \times 43/150 \approx 163.4\,K$ and $891 \times 29/150 \approx 172.3\,Q\setminus K$; harmonic factor ratio $\sqrt{(1/163.4 + 1/172.3)/(1/43 + 1/29)} = 0.4544$), $\text{SE}_{\text{full}} \approx 0.0426\text{--}0.0493$ gives a two-sided $\alpha = 0.05$, 80%-power minimum detectable effect of $\text{MDE}_{80\%} = 2.8016 \times \text{SE}_{\text{full}} \approx 0.119\text{--}0.138$. However, the 1,461 Gate 3 families are a fixed finite catalogue (of which the 300-family pilot already sampled $26.3\%$ of $K$ and $16.8\%$ of $Q \setminus K$).
   * **Recommendation:** Either record the explicit variance-scaling formula and finite-population caveat or remove the uncomputed power claim when reporting the full Gate 3 census.
3. **O3 (Scope of `0 / 30` Mobilome Keyword Flag & Housekeeping/Chromosomal Characterization; `scripts/03_analyse_conservation.py:99-116,189-197`, `DOSSIER.md:166,226`):**
   * `best_ko_mobilome_flag` tests an 11-keyword substring list (`MOBILOME_TERMS`) only on flanks carrying `best_recurrent_ko` (`0 / 30` positive). Across all `296` Tier 1 flanks of those 30 conserved families, `141 / 296 (47.6%)` have no KEGG KO (`mapped_noKO`) and `172 / 296 (58.1%)` have Bakta product `"hypothetical protein"`. Among the 30 top recurrent KOs themselves (`DOSSIER.md:170-199`), 8 have Bakta product `"hypothetical protein"`, 6 have eggNOG COG category `S` (function unknown, plus `K06888` `DUF255`), `K00590` (`F31Dr2::F31Dr2_428982_6`) is a `DNA methylase`, and an adjacent `mapped_noKO` flank in `F38Sr1::F38Sr1_386592_2` (`2481_0`, `p2`) carries eggNOG description `"COG2931 RTX toxins and related Ca2 -binding proteins"`.
   * **Recommendation:** Describe `0 / 30` strictly as absence of phage/transposase/integrase keywords among the 30 top recurrent KO annotations, rather than claiming it proves complete absence of mobile elements or that all 30 contexts are "core housekeeping and metabolic genes" (`DOSSIER.md:226`).
4. **O4 (Minor Documentation Alignment of Multiplicity Tie-Breaker Key; `DESIGN.md:75`, `DOSSIER.md:56`, `scripts/02_extract_neighbourhoods.py:838-847`):**
   * `scripts/02_extract_neighbourhoods.py:838-847` sorts candidate loci per `(family, species)` by `(-eligible_locus_primary, -eligible_locus_stop3_sens, -n_exact_mapped_flanks, -n_stop3_or_exact_mapped_flanks, -n_present_flanks, -contig_len_bp, -total_genes_on_contig, member)` (all strictly outcome-independent), whereas `DESIGN.md:75` and `DOSSIER.md:56` show slightly abbreviated versions of this tuple. Align the prose formula with the exact 8-tuple in the code.

## 6. Final Verdict

**VIABLE.** All five prior critical flaws (`C1`–`C5`) are resolved and verified against the frozen v3 code, checksum-pinned inputs (`3,800` files), and IBEX Slurm outputs (`53712511`/`53713357`). Both the positive within-arm positional-reference enrichment ($p = 0.0002$ in both $K$ and $Q \setminus K$) and the null covariate-adjusted between-arm contrast ($\Delta_{\text{MH}}(E_{\text{fam}}) = -0.0648, p = 0.4907$) are identifiable, reproducible, and falsifiable within the explicitly bounded 90%-cluster-representative multi-species population.
