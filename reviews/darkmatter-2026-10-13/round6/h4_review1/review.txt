1. EXECUTIVE VERDICT & BOUNDED HYPOTHESIS
Status: REVISE

Bounded Hypothesis:
Among confidence-qualified sequence-dark representative protein models (length >= 100 aa, mean pLDDT >= 0.70; 1 model per fold cluster) with low whole-chain PDB structural similarity (qTM_wc < 0.5), does physical cropping and de novo Foldseek alignment of outcome-independent confident structural regions (>= 50 aa, 15-aa rolling-window smoothed pLDDT >= 0.70, bridging <= 5-aa dips with mean pLDDT >= 0.55) recover reference-conditional matches (qTM_crop >= 0.5) at a higher model-level rate in candidates with whole-chain local hits (K_local) than in candidates without local hits (K_dual_none), evaluated against internal fold-matched dark comparators (Q \ K) and homology-filtered PDB positive controls?

Scope and Feasibility Assessment:
All inputs in INPUTS.tsv are accessible on IBEX and SHA-256 verified. Relative to round5/EXTENSIONS.md (§4, lines 102–128) and superseded V1 (results/v1_superseded/README.md, lines 1–12), V2 narrows scope from unsupported claims of "bona fide novel domains", "intrinsic disorder", and "linker reorientation" to a reference-conditional, outcome-blind cropped-region recovery estimand (DESIGN.md §1.2, lines 23–34; DOSSIER.md §4, lines 150–160). V2 physically crops PDB coordinates and runs de novo Foldseek easy-search (scripts/v2_crop_and_search_regions.py, lines 155–173, 361–378; Slurm job 53700779, 12 s walltime for 300 models / 413 regions), proving full-cohort feasibility (N=5,851, ~5 min on 32 cores) and establishing the disconnected-maxima audit (82.14% target discordance between best_qtm and best_alntm across 2,816 retrieved candidates; scripts/01_audit_exact_hits_and_leakage.py, lines 174–224; results/exact_hit_vs_maxima.tsv). However, five critical flaws require revision before VIABLE status.

2. ADJUDICATION OF ROOT AUDIT NOTES & CRITICAL ISSUES

C1. Post-hoc sequence-identity filtering of `--max-seqs 50` output conflates hit-list saturation with structural match failure in controls (Tiers 2–4).
- Evidence: scripts/v2_crop_and_search_regions.py, lines 371, 437–446, 472–480; results/v2_region_evaluations.tsv, lines 2–415; DOSSIER.md lines 36–39, 136–144, 198, 202.
- Impact: Foldseek runs with `--max-seqs 50` (line 371), and Tiers 2–4 (`fident < 0.30, 0.25, 0.20`) filter that 50-hit list post hoc (line 437). When all returned hits exceed the cutoff, `t2_top` is `None`, and lines 472–480 record `t2_qtm = 0.0` and `t2_matched = 0`. Across the 103 control regions in `results/v2_region_evaluations.tsv`:
  * Tier 1 (source-PDB excluded): 100 matched (`qTM >= 0.5`), 2 low-score with hit (`0 < qTM < 0.5`), 0 post-filter empty hit list, 1 raw zero retrieval.
  * Tier 2 (`fident < 0.30`): 83 matched, 2 low-score with hit, 17 post-filter empty hit list — 17/17 (100%) have `total_hits_raw == 50` (e.g., `KF000066_reg1`, `KF000126_reg1`).
  * Tier 3 (`fident < 0.25`): 78 matched, 2 low-score with hit, 22 post-filter empty hit list (21/22 with `total_hits_raw == 50`, 1 with 42).
  * Tier 4 (`fident < 0.20`): 70 matched, 5 low-score with hit, 27 post-filter empty hit list (26/27 with `total_hits_raw == 50`, 1 with 42).
  Conditional match rates among control regions retaining >= 1 hit are 98.04% (100/102, Tier 1), 97.65% (83/85, Tier 2), 97.50% (78/80, Tier 3), and 93.33% (70/75, Tier 4). The reported control drop (`97.1% -> 80.6% -> 75.7% -> 68.0%`) in Go Criterion 2 (DOSSIER.md line 198) is an artifact of `--max-seqs 50` saturation by close homologs rather than TM-score failure below 0.5.
- Repair: Record `t{k}_remaining_hits` in region/model tables to separate `POST_EXCLUSION_NO_REMAINING_HIT` from `PERSISTENT_LOW_SCORE`, report conditional match rates among units with remaining hits, and increase `--max-seqs` (e.g., `1000` or `2000`) so close homologs do not saturate the hit list before `<25%` identity targets are evaluated.

C2. Models with zero eligible confident regions (`n_confident_regions == 0`) are misclassified as Foldseek `ZERO_RETRIEVAL`.
- Evidence: scripts/v2_crop_and_search_regions.py, lines 314–329, 497–521; results/v2_cropped_regions_manifest.tsv; results/v2_model_summaries.tsv (line 46 `41PRr3::41PRr3_702372_18`); DOSSIER.md lines 62, 120–121, 159.
- Impact: Eleven pilot models (`3 K_local`, `4 K_dual_none`, `4 Q_not_K`) have zero confident regions (`>= 50` aa, smoothed pLDDT `>= 0.70`) and generate no cropped PDB (`cropped_pdb = "NONE"`). Lines 503–518 set `reg_has_retrieval = False` when `n_regs == 0` and assign `trans_cat = "ZERO_RETRIEVAL"`. Thus, 3/5 of `K_local` and 4/15 of `K_dual_none` `ZERO_RETRIEVAL` models in Table 3.2A were never searched by Foldseek. Furthermore, DOSSIER.md line 159 mislabels these model rates (30.0% and 5.0%) as region rates and attributes them to Foldseek `3Di/AA k-mer prefilters (-s 9.5, --max-seqs 50)`. Among region-eligible models (`n_confident_regions >= 1`), conditional recovery is `70/97 (72.16%)` in `K_local` vs `15/46 (32.61%)` in `K_dual_none`.
- Repair: Separate `NO_CONFIDENT_REGION` (`n_confident_regions == 0`) from `ZERO_RETRIEVAL` (`n_confident_regions >= 1` and `total_hits_raw == 0`), report both unconditional and region-eligible conditional recovery proportions, and fix DOSSIER.md line 159.

C3. Exclusion-tier and reference-database asymmetry between whole-chain baseline and cropped evaluation.
- Evidence: scripts/v2_crop_and_search_regions.py, lines 229–235, 244–246, 491–524; results/v2_model_summaries.tsv; results/control_leakage_audit.tsv; IBEX `/ibex/scratch/projects/c2014/rob/metagenomics/04_structure/00_structure_clean/scripts/05c_screen.sh`, lines 26, 53–59.
- Impact:
  1. Read-only check of `05c_screen.sh` confirms the historical PDB whole-chain search used identical Foldseek version (`10-941cd33`), database (`pdb_20260730/foldseek_pdb`), and flags (`--alignment-type 1 -e 10 -s 9.5 --max-seqs 50`) as V2 crops. However, `05c_screen.sh` omitted `fident`, and line 492 sets `wc_qtm = m["best_pdb_qtm"]` (Tier 0 unfiltered) while evaluating crops at Tier 1 (`reg_t1_max_qtm`, source-PDB excluded) and Tiers 2–4 (`fident` filtered). In `control_leakage_audit.tsv`, 42/3,013 controls have Tier 0 `unfiltered_pdb_qtm >= 0.5` solely via self-hits (`top_non_self_qtm < 0.5`); running `--mode full` misclassifies those 42 controls as `WHOLE_CHAIN_MATCHED`.
  2. Strata `K_local`, `K_dual_none`, and `Q_not_K` are defined on dual-reference (PDB + AFDB) scores (lines 229–235, 244–246), whereas `wc_qtm` and cropped searches use PDB only. Consequently, 10/100 pilot `K_local` models (87/936 full) have `whole_chain_pdb_alntm < 0.5` (AFDB-only local hit; e.g., `V12Sr1::V12Sr1_267603_7` has `whole_chain_pdb_alntm = 0.0`), and 20/75 pilot `Q_not_K` models (700/1,807 full) have `whole_chain_pdb_qtm < 0.5` (AFDB-only match), causing Table 3.2A to report 14/75 (18.7%) of `Q_not_K` as "recovered after cropping" in PDB.
  3. Because `if wc_qtm < 0.5:` (line 515) branches first, all 55 `Q_not_K` models with `wc_qtm >= 0.5` are labeled `WHOLE_CHAIN_MATCHED`, masking 5/55 models (4 with `n_confident_regions == 0` and `V34Dr2::V34Dr2_233315_1` with `wc_qtm = 0.6832 -> reg_t1_max_qtm = 0.4161`) that lost their match upon cropping.
- Repair: Pin `05c_screen.sh` provenance; apply matched exclusion tiers to whole-chain and cropped metrics (using `top_non_self_qtm` for Tier 1 controls, or re-running whole-chain PDB search with `fident`); stratify or restrict `K_local` (`best_pdb_alntm >= 0.5`, n=849) and `Q \ K` (`best_pdb_qtm >= 0.5`, n=1,107) by PDB baseline status; and record crop retention vs loss for baseline-matched models.

C4. Unweighted length-stratified pilot estimator for `K_local` vs unstratified comparators.
- Evidence: scripts/v2_crop_and_search_regions.py, lines 254–263, 565; DESIGN.md line 82; DOSSIER.md lines 33, 120, 197.
- Impact: Pilot `K_local` ($N=100$) uses equal allocation of 25 models across fixed length bins `[100,200), [200,300), [300,400), [400,10000)` (mislabeled as "quartiles" at line 254), whereas other strata use simple random sampling. In the full `K_local` cohort ($N=936$), bin shares are 13.68% (128), 46.47% (435), 24.68% (231), and 15.17% (142), with pilot recovery rates of 48.0% (12/25), 60.0% (15/25), 80.0% (20/25), and 92.0% (23/25). The Horvitz–Thompson design-weighted recovery estimate for `K_local` is **68.15%** (`27.36%` persistent low score, `2.95%` no confident region, `1.53%` zero retrieval), whereas DOSSIER.md reports only the unweighted mean (70.0%) and DESIGN.md omits the length stratification.
- Repair: Disclose length-stratified sampling in DESIGN.md and report the Horvitz–Thompson weighted estimator (68.15%) alongside unweighted counts, or execute the full census (`--mode full`, N=5,851).

C5. Mislabeling of query span as aligned residue count and factual/statistical reporting errors in DOSSIER.md.
- Evidence: scripts/v2_crop_and_search_regions.py, lines 408, 462–463, 470; scripts/01_audit_exact_hits_and_leakage.py, line 8; results/v2_crop_pdb_hits.tsv; results/v2_region_evaluations.tsv (line 34); DOSSIER.md lines 40, 108, 131–136, 145, 198.
- Impact:
  1. Line 408 sets `qalnlen = qend - qstart + 1` (query span), whereas 9,516/11,247 crop hits (84.61%) have `alnlen > tspan` (query insertions opposite target gaps; true aligned pairs = `qspan + tspan - alnlen`, e.g., row 1 `25Sr1::25Sr1_59902_2_reg1` has `qlen=91, qspan=87` [span cov 0.956], `tspan=114, alnlen=128`, giving `73` aligned pairs [aligned-residue cov 0.802]).
  2. DOSSIER.md line 108 reports `p = 0.010, McNemar's test on paired exclusions` (`117/3,013` vs `159/3,013`). In `control_leakage_audit.tsv`, paired discordant counts are $b = 0, c = 42$ (exact McNemar $p = 4.55 \times 10^{-13}$); $p = 0.010$ is an unpaired two-sample $\chi^2$ test ($p = 0.00965$) mislabeled as McNemar's test.
  3. DOSSIER.md line 198 tests region-level proportions (`78/103` vs `19/60`), violating DESIGN.md line 44 (model is primary unit; model-level Tier 3 is `56/75 = 74.67%` vs `15/50 = 30.00%`, Fisher's exact $p = 1.10 \times 10^{-6}$), and `v2_model_summaries.tsv` omits Tiers 3–4.
  4. DOSSIER.md lines 40 and 145 falsely state all candidate hits have sequence identity `< 20%` (`v2_region_evaluations.tsv` has 3 candidate top hits `>= 0.20`, including `27Dr3::27Dr3_493525_1_reg1` at `0.240`, and `v2_crop_pdb_hits.tsv` has 97 candidate rows `>= 0.20`), and Table 3.2B averages `t1_fident` across zero-retrieval regions coded as `0.0` (dragging `K_dual_none` mean `fident` from `6.12%` among retrieved regions to `4.5%`).
- Repair: Compute aligned residue pairs (`qspan + tspan - alnlen`) or explicitly label `qcov` as span coverage; report exact paired discordant counts ($b=0, c=42$); aggregate Tiers 3–4 to the model level in `v2_model_summaries.tsv` and test Go Criterion 2 on models; and correct the sequence-identity prose and retrieved-only mean `fident` in DOSSIER.md.
