# Editorial and Scientific-Fidelity Review: Dark-Matter Biology Rewrite
**Reviewer:** Gemini 4 Argon (High)
**Date:** 15 October 2026
**Scope:** Read-only comparison of `manuscript/main.tex` and `manuscript/authors.tex` against `reviews/darkmatter-biology-rewrite-20261015/baseline.tex` and `reviews/darkmatter-family-followup-20261015/RESULTS_FOR_AUTHOR.txt`.

## Overall Assessment
The rewrite successfully transforms the manuscript from a defensive methodological census into a question-led biological study focused on lineage structure, subsurface Thermoplasmatota ecology, cross-soil repertoire sharing, structural domain recovery, and heliorhodopsin diversity. Evidence strength is properly calibrated throughout: no claims of experimentally proven function, adaptive selection, desert endemism, novel protein folds, or t-SNE-derived novelty are made. `manuscript/authors.tex` matches the 19 authors and seven affiliations of `lifeonair/manuscript/authors.tex` in the exact instructed order (Alejandra Lopez Velazquez first, middle authors alphabetical by surname, Magnus Rueping and Robert Hoehndorf last) with no shared-first-author claim.

However, moving material out of Results introduced several broken cross-references, a split-paragraph orphan in Methods, missing denominators/outcomes for analyses still defined in Methods, and a few undefined terms or splice errors.

The larger sulfur count after aggregation..."`) to the end of the chemistry paragraph in `Supplementary Note S3` (after line 881, following the `54,227` sulfur result).
