Approved execution scope, 2026-10-15

Run F1 photolyase/cryptochrome and F2 compatible-solute phosphatase feasibility
first; advance technically defensible tracks to full analyses. Then evaluate
F3 rhodopsins, F4 EctC and F5 carbonic anhydrases the same way. These IDs are
separate from H1-H4. Preserve the manuscript until author sees results and
both root's and Argon's judgement.

Promotion gate concerns gene/family reliability and a meaningful comparison,
not a favourable p-value or attractive projection. Full analyses may yield
negative or inconclusive biological findings. Preserve every screened track.

Root audit requirements:
* Input inventory and actual scheduler exit state; only worker execution.
* Current-catalogue membership, exact protein-sequence joins and denominator
  closures; explicitly label historical inputs, missing files and attrition.
* Reference provenance, function evidence and competitor-family coverage;
  plausible family assignment separated from confirmed biochemical activity.
* Sequence completeness/read support or explicit missing evidence; no broad
  coding inference from historical dN/dS or decoder scores.
* Raw search rows independently summarised; sequence identity and coverage
  interpreted at the aligned/domain/full-chain level as appropriate.
* Full-space embedding outputs and actual GPU execution checked; t-SNE/UMAP
  used for visualisation, with taxonomy/length/reference-density controls.
* Host/site/expedition dependence and prior exploration handled; no testing
  numerous habitat axes then reporting only the favourable one.
* Claims distinguish reference-relative novelty, family identity, ecological
  association, expression, biochemical function and adaptive benefit.
* Exact code/inputs/software/parameters/seeds/job IDs/hashes retained on IBEX,
  GPU artifacts retained on ORIX and mirrored or mapped with checksums.
* Independent reproduction of key result tables from raw results in Slurm;
  checksums alone count as integrity verification, not analytic replication.
* Argon receives results and reproducibility audit, including failed gates,
  negative outcomes and missing evidence; asks whether results justify paper
  inclusion and what exact biological statement each can support.

Manuscript baseline hashes are stored in manuscript_baseline.sha256. Existing
uncommitted manuscript modifications predate this task and must be preserved.
