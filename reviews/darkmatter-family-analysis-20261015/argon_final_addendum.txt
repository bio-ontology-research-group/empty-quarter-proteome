2. EXPLICIT CORRECTIONS TO PRIOR REPORT (argon_f345_results.txt)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rather than rewriting `argon_f345_results.txt`, we record four explicit corrections:
1. Exact HMM Profile Identifiers (F1 & F5):
   - `argon_f345_results.txt` misattributed F5 $\gamma$-CA and `CsoSCA` to `PF00132`
     and `PF08936`, and omitted `PF04244` from the F1 table. In
     `10_f5_carbonic_anhydrase_full.py`, target models are `PF00484` ($\beta$-CA),
     **`NF050158`/`NF040597`** ($\gamma$-CA), and **`TIGR02701`** (`CsoSCA`), whereas
     **`PF00132` (`Hexapep`) and `PF00583` (`Acetyltransf_1`)** are competing
     left-handed $\beta$-helix (LbH) negative controls (`margin >= 10.0` bits).
     The F1 CPF census includes **`PF04244`** alongside `PF00875` and `PF03441`.
2. Over-interpretation of F3 Taxonomic & Neighborhood Evidence:
   - `argon_f345_results.txt` called the `40/42` `Halobacteriota` Type-1
     transducer loci *"canonical haloarchaeal Sensory Rhodopsin I/II (`SR-I`/`SR-II`)
     paired with cognate `Htr` transducers"*. In fact, `Halobacteriota` taxonomy
     plus adjacent `MCP/HAMP/CheW` domains is **compatible with known haloarchaeal
     sensory architecture**, not proof of canonical `SR/Htr` identity or phototaxis.
     Marker absence in the remaining `98` Type-1 or `65` Heliorhodopsin loci does
     not assign them as ion pumps or refute sensing. For the strict 1-per-MAG
     all-profile Type-1 context test, two-class `Holm p = 0.012695` (raw `p = 0.006348`) governs.
3. Precision on F4/F5 Cluster Means & Scope of Negative Claims:
   - For F5 `GammaCA` equal-addition (`n = 100`) 50%-clusters, EQ (`34.07`) is
     numerically **slightly higher** than SMAG (`32.36`) and GEM (`32.77`), not
     *"no higher"*; this slight difference does not establish exceptional breadth
     under taxonomic/architectural confounding. F5 biochemical/thermal properties
     are **untested, not failed**. F4 results show cohort-wide exceptional `EctC`
     divergence is not established (`median 68.3%` identity to curated refs), not
     that zero individual divergent `EctC` candidates exist.
4. Governance, Ecology & Synteny Terminology:
   - Analyses followed a **prespecified analysis plan**, not formal preregistration.
     F1 surface enrichment is an observational association, not selection or
     causality. F4 `EctABC`/`EctABCD` clusters (`50%` profile-coverage gate on
