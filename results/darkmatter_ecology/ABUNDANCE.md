# B3d — the dark proteome is abundant and prevalent (not rare contamination/relic DNA)

CoverM read-recruitment of 23,382 dark families across 551 samples. Contamination / dead-DNA would be sporadic and trace-level; the data show the opposite.

## Prevalence (widely detected, not sporadic)
- median dark family detected in **52 / 551 samples** (9%).
- **7.9%** of families are present in ≥50% of samples; 48.4% in ≥10%.
- only 1.3% are detected in ≤1 sample (the contamination-like tail).

## Abundance magnitude (genuine, not trace)
- median peak (max across samples) abundance **584.8**; **89.4%** of families peak ≥100 and 99.5% peak ≥10.
- median mean-where-present abundance 37.29; 100.0% have mean-present ≥1.
- **7.9%** are BOTH prevalent (≥50% of samples) AND non-trace (peak ≥10) — the joint signature of a real, dominant community component, not a rare artefact.
