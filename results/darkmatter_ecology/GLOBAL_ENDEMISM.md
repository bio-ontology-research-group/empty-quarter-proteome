**Status:** historical descriptive screen; not promoted as an endemism or selection result.

- Sequence-dark families searched: 23,382 (all were already no-hits in the frozen UniRef50 screen).
- Families with a detected MGnify90 homolog under the historical rule: 14,830 (63.4%).
- Families with no detected MGnify90 homolog under that rule: 8,552 (36.6%).

The no-hit class is **MGnify90-relative**. It does not establish geographic absence, desert specificity, or endemism.

Historical unclustered diagnostic: median pairwise NG86 dN/dS 0.393 in the no-hit class versus 0.390 in the hit class (Mann--Whitney p=7.09e-03). This is descriptive and does not establish a difference in evolutionary constraint or selection.
Historical within-EQ occupancy diagnostic: median samples/sites present 41 in the MGnify90 no-hit class versus 62 in the hit class.
