# RQ-G2 — Who feeds whom at biome scale (scale the cross-feeding)?

**Status:** 🟡 answerable now (compute-heavy)

## Answer
The 16-sample RQ-G1 pilot demonstrates technical feasibility only. Biome-scale gapseq+SMETANA inference has not
been run and would require a predeclared sampling design, completeness controls and substantial IBEX compute.
