# RQ-B6 — Are endemic genes more or less constrained than cosmopolitan ones?

**Status:** historical descriptive screen; not promoted as a constraint comparison

## Answer
In the archived, unclustered diagnostic, site-restricted families (≤3 of 60 sites) had median pairwise NG86 dN/dS 0.332, versus 0.420 for EQ-wide families (≥30 sites); Spearman(n_sites, dN/dS)=+0.18. The nominal Mann--Whitney p-value is retained in the source table, but orthogroup/pair dependence and unequal support are not modelled. The result therefore does **not** show that one class is under stronger evolutionary constraint. The occupancy labels are within-EQ descriptions, not evidence that either class is endemic or cosmopolitan.
