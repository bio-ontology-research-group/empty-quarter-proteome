# RQ-C3 — Is predictability worse for more phylogenetically novel taxa?

**Status:** ✅ answered

## Answer
**Yes.** The KO-space ceiling (fraction of proteins that any 16S predictor could see) rises with GTDB novelty — the more novel the lineage, the larger the unpredictable gene fraction.

**Evidence / reproduce:** `figures/fig_picrust2_bias.pdf` panel c; `code/make_picrust2_bias_figure.py`.

**References:** douglas2020picrust2; matchado2024limits (keys in `manuscript/references.bib`).
