# RQ-B2 — Is EQ dark matter endemic/desert-specific, or just globally undersampled?

**Status:** ✅ answered

## Answer
**Database-relative specificity remains substantial.** Under one search rule, 36.6% of dark families have no detected MGnify90 homolog, 47.4% have no GEM homolog, 31.9% have no homolog in the union of five desert catalogues, and **21.4% have no detected homolog in the named MGnify90, GEM and five-desert comparison**. These no-hits prioritise broader searches but do not establish geographic absence or endemism.
