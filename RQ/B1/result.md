# RQ-B1 — Is dark-proteome composition environmentally structured beyond space?

**Status:** ✅ answered

## Answer
**Yes.** PERMANOVA shows compartment, climate|space and geochem|space each explain significant variance in dark-family composition *beyond* the spatial term; distance-decay Mantel r = +0.16 (p=0.001).

**Evidence / reproduce:** `results/darkmatter_ecology/phase_b_summary.txt`; `code/phase_b_ecology.py`. (See also RQ-B7.)

**References:** fierer2017soil; maestre2015aridity (keys in `manuscript/references.bib`).
