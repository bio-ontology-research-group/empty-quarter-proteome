# RQ-H2 — How does EQ compare to other hot/cold deserts?

**Status:** ✅ answered relative to named comparison catalogues

## Answer
Under one historical DIAMOND rule, no-hit fractions against individual named desert catalogues ranged from 42--89% (Atacama 89%, Antarctic 56%, Mojave 52%, arid-soil comparator 42%), and 31.9% of EQ dark families had no detected homolog in the union of the five named desert catalogues. These are database-relative detection results; they do not establish universal desert uniqueness or geographic absence.
