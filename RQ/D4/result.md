# RQ-D4 — How is UV/desiccation survival encoded, and does it partition by sun exposure?

**Status:** ✅ prevalence census

## Answer
**Repair-marker capacity is widespread and photolyase prevalence differs by compartment.** NER 99.4%, BER 99.5% and NHEJ 78.4% of MAGs; photolyase-marker prevalence is 22.4% in surface versus 13.1% in deep samples. The observational contrast does not by itself establish sun exposure as the cause.
