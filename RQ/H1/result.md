# RQ-H1 — Could the database-relative dark signal reflect contamination, kit-ome, or relic DNA?

**Status:** partial controls only; exclusion analysis remains pending

## Answer
A historical named-pathogen census and read-recruitment summaries are reassuring but do not exclude reagent
contamination or relic DNA. A formal negative-control/decontam analysis using SNTC/SPositive controls and the
staged relic-DNA workflow is required before a contamination-exclusion claim. Database-relative no-hits are not
evidence of endemism.

**References:** salter2014contamination; davis2018decontam (keys in `manuscript/references.bib`).
