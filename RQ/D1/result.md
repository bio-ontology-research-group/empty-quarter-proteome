# RQ-D1 — Is the community metabolically 'living on air' (atmospheric H2/CO)?

**Status:** ✅ genomic-capacity census

## Answer
**Trace-gas oxidation capacity is widespread.** 3,386 MAGs carry high-affinity atmospheric [NiFe]-hydrogenase markers (groups 1l+1h) and 3,828 carry form-I CO dehydrogenase markers; **1,204 MAGs (~8%)** carry both. These genomic markers support metabolic potential, not a direct rate or activity measurement.

**Evidence / reproduce:** `results/atmospheric/PHYLOGENY.md`; `reproducibility/verify.py` (`a1.*`). Fig `figures/fig_lifeonair_by_phylum.pdf`.
