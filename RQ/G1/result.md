# RQ-G1 — Is the community competition- or cooperation-structured?

**Status:** ✅ answered

## Answer
**Competition-leaning, function broadly distributed.** SMETANA global MRO ≈ 0.7 (metabolic-resource overlap = competition signal); cross-feeding is present but **not guild-anchored** — the atmospheric guild are not preferential metabolite donors (guild ≈ non-guild, p=0.69, robust to a completeness control).
