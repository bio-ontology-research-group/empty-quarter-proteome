# RQ-D5 — Are compatible-solute (osmolyte) strategies convergent and salinity-linked?

**Status:** historical exploratory association; not a selection result

## Answer
Trehalose-, glucosylglycerol- and ectoine-linked annotations recur across multiple dark families, and the archived partial-correlation screen placed some families preferentially in saline/rhizosphere samples. Osmolyte-linked families were not enriched as a class (OR 1.10, nominal p=0.45). This exploratory association does not establish convergence, causation, or evolutionary selection; a current-catalogue, family-cluster-aware dose--response analysis would be required for those claims.

**Evidence / reproduce:** `results/darkmatter_ecology/OSMOLYTE.md`; `code/phase_e_partial_osmolyte.py`. (Dose-response deepening: see RQ-B3.)
