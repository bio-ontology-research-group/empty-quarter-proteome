# RQ-A2 — Is the dark proteome real (selected genes) or spurious ORFs?

**Status:** ✅ answered

## Answer
**The recurrent, alignable subset carries coding-like evidence.** Its filtered pairwise NG86 median is **0.391**, and read recruitment finds only 1.3% in a single sample while 99.5% reach peak abundance ≥10. Pairwise within-population dN/dS is descriptive and is not a direct estimate of long-term selection; these results do not authenticate every predicted ORF.
