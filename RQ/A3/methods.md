**Question.** Does the dual-database structural-novelty signal persist when
ESMFold confidence and protein length are taken into account?

## Background

The original structural pipeline predicted 3Di strings from sequence with
ProstT5 and searched them against AlphaFold-DB with FoldSeek. Representatives
with no AFDB hit were candidate novel folds. RQ-A3 re-tests those candidates
using explicit ESMFold coordinates, searches both AFDB and the curated PDB,
and reports sensitivity to model confidence and length. A no-hit call from a
predicted representation is not itself a validated novel fold.

## Inputs

With `M=/ibex/scratch/projects/c2014/rob/metagenomics`:

- `$M/06_esmfold_novelfold/pdb/plddt_shard*.tsv`: `rep`, protein length,
  mean pLDDT on a 0–1 scale, and model status.
- `RQ/A3/best_afdb_tm.tsv`: best AFDB TM score per representative from the
  explicit-coordinate FoldSeek/TMalign search.
- `RQ/A3/best_pdb_tm.tsv`: best curated-PDB TM score per representative from
  the same search procedure.

The AFDB and PDB searches use FoldSeek `--alignment-type 1` (TMalign), `-e 10`,
and `--max-seqs 50`. The per-query tables retain the maximum `alntmscore`.
The AFDB result was recovered from the completed result database after the
original job timed out during `convertalis`; the structural search itself was
not rerun.

## Analysis universe and dual-novel definition

Concatenate the pLDDT shards, ignore repeated headers, and retain records with
`status=ok` and numeric pLDDT. Records marked `too_long` have no model-quality
value and are excluded. Join the scored representative identifiers to each
best-TM table. A missing search row is assigned TM=0 because it represents no
detected database hit.

A representative is **dual-novel** when:

```text
best_AFDB_TM < 0.5 AND best_PDB_TM < 0.5
```

This is a candidate call, not a statement that the fold has been
experimentally or computationally validated as new.

## Confidence sensitivity

Recompute the dual-novel numerator, retained-model denominator, and rate at
inclusive mean-pLDDT thresholds 0.00, 0.40, 0.50, 0.60, 0.70, and 0.80. Rates,
not only counts, are required because progressively stricter thresholds retain
progressively smaller populations. Report the pLDDT ≥0.80 result with its
denominator rather than treating its small count as a corrected floor.

Compute median pLDDT and median length separately for dual-novel
representatives and representatives with at least one TM≥0.5 database match.

## Length sensitivity

Within the pLDDT ≥0.60 subset, calculate the dual-novel rate in non-overlapping
length bins:

```text
<100, 100–<150, 150–<250, 250–<400, and ≥400 aa
```

Also count dual-novel representatives that meet pLDDT ≥0.60 and length ≥100
aa. TM-align performance on short structures and database coverage of small
proteins are not separable in this analysis.

## Historical missing control (now resolved)

This historical snapshot lacked a known-fold control. The current analysis now
applies the same length, confidence, reference and score-normalisation rules to
3,013 known-fold controls and reports metric-specific false-no-hit rates. The
candidate and control cohorts remain unmatched for length, partial status and
prediction confidence, so the comparison is descriptive and no no-hit count is
a validated novel-fold count.

## Reproduction

On IBEX:

```bash
M=/ibex/scratch/projects/c2014/rob/metagenomics
python3 RQ/A3/finalize_tm.py \
  --plddt "$M"/06_esmfold_novelfold/pdb/plddt_shard*.tsv
python3 RQ/A3/run.py
```

`finalize_tm.py` writes `fold_validation_summary.tsv` and the confidence/length
sensitivity figure `fig_fold_validation.{pdf,png}`.
